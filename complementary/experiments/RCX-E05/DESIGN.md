## 0. Summary

On RCX's exact Blocksworld state space for n = 7 (65,990 states, `rcx/bw/space.py`):
- a goal is a **weight vector w over 56 observable fact features** (on, ontable, clear) plus a step cost;
- an episode ends with a STOP action that collects w·φ(s_final), and each move costs c;
- every goal-weighted optimal value V\*_w is computed **exactly** by max-plus value iteration over the enumerated graph.

A goal-conditioned successor-feature (SF) network ψθ(s, z) is trained once on exact labels from a library of 64
training goal weights. For an unseen weight w′ it re-ranks candidate moves zero-shot by generalized policy
improvement (GPI):

  Q(s, a) = max over z ∈ L ∪ {w′} of w̄′·[(0, 1) + ψθ(s′, z)],   with w̄′ = (w′, −c′).

The two primary comparisons are:
- **P1:** against a matched-capacity scalar goal-conditioned value V_θ(s, w) (UVFA) trained on the same labels;
- **P2:** against per-goal value regression trained on fresh labels of each held-out goal.

Strong non-learned baselines and oracle ceilings bracket both. The primary endpoint is normalized closed-loop regret
on three held-out goal families. The total cost is about 45 CPU-min at ≤ 4 threads and < 1.5 GB RSS, run in short
phases that yield to E01R. **Feasibility: FEASIBLE.** Everything is generated by an exact oracle that is already on
disk, and no external or peer data is needed (§7).

## 1. Hypothesis, complementarity and tiers

### 1.1 Falsifiable hypothesis

**H-E05.** The domain has fixed dynamics, and preferences are linear in observable fact features. Here a
goal-conditioned successor-feature critic ψθ(s, z) is trained once on exact labels from 64 training goal weights and
composed by GPI. On held-out goal weights it achieves:
- **(P1)** lower normalized closed-loop regret than a matched-capacity scalar goal-conditioned value V_θ(s, w) (UVFA)
  trained on the *identical* (state, weight) label set, by at least 0.05 (macro over the three primary families);
- **(P2)** regret non-inferior, within a margin of 0.05, to a per-goal value regressor trained on 400 fresh exact labels
  *of each held-out goal*.

The SF critic needs **zero** labels per new goal.

**Falsifiers.** H-E05 is refuted at this tier by either of:
- the P1 confidence interval excluding a practical advantage (upper bound < 0.05);
- SF regret exceeding the per-goal regressor's by more than the margin.

The **mechanism itself** is refuted for this domain if the exact (learning-free) GPI ceiling over the library has
normalized regret ≥ 0.5 on the primary families (§6.5).

### 1.2 Why this complements Rineya and its world models

| Existing component | What it gives | What it lacks, and what E05 adds |
|---|---|---|
| BWM F (LM forward model) plus `plan_beam` (frozen `18a5f22b`) | One-step imagined transitions, ranked by unmet goal facts for a fixed **hard** goal | No notion of preference weights. A new objective needs a new heuristic or retraining. Every step of lookahead is an LM call, and errors compound. |
| RCWM consequence models (T2, T2b) | One-step state deltas under a (fixed) rule | No multi-step, policy-conditional consequences |
| RCX R-04/R-07 GNN value and A3 hybrid (B4 arm a) | Distance to a fixed **hard** conjunctive goal | One value per goal definition. Soft priorities, penalties and trade-offs are not representable. |
| BWM value LoRA (B4 arm b, BWM-owned) | Model-native h\* for hard goals | Same limit; it is hard-goal only |

**What SF adds.** It gives a **policy-conditional, multi-step consequence summary**: "if I pursue goal z from here, which
facts will hold at the end, and after how many steps". This summary is **re-weightable at zero cost** for any new
preference vector over the same features.

**Why this matters for Rineya.** An assistant's objectives change per user and task (priorities, things to avoid,
cost of effort) while the world's dynamics stay fixed, which is exactly the SF regime (P04, Barreto et al.). Per-goal
critics do not scale to that. A one-step forward model would have to re-plan from scratch for every preference change.

E05 asks whether this interface is *learnable and transferable* at small scale before anyone spends GPU on it.

### 1.3 What a positive result would and would not establish

A positive result (ADVANCE) **would establish, at mechanism tier (tier-B specialist, exact dynamics, n = 7):**
- A learned SF critic composed by GPI transfers zero-shot to unseen preference weights inside the feature span.
- It beats a scalar goal-conditioned critic trained on the same oracle labels.
- It matches per-goal critics that need fresh labels for every new goal.
- A measured **label exchange rate** k\*: the per-goal label budget a scalar critic needs to match it.
- The attribution controls (§5.2) would also say whether the gain comes from linear recomposition at test time (GPI)
  or merely from the richer vector supervision.

It **would not establish:**
- any Rineya native ability (no Rineya weights are involved);
- transfer across object counts or domains (n is fixed at 7);
- robustness to LM-imagined states (BWM's F) or LM-proposed candidates (here candidates are exact-legal successors);
- value for **hard conjunctive goals**, BWM's main regime. These are outside the linear span, and E05 expects SF to
  fail there (H-CONJ);
- robustness to changed dynamics (H-DYN, where E05 again expects failure);
- that BWM's beam or any LM planner gains;
- the bundle's "+5 pp goal success at matched compute" target. That is a planner-level claim for E06 and for the native
  path (§8).

### 1.4 Tier ladder for this line of work

| Rung | E05 content | Claim allowed |
|---|---|---|
| **Instrument** | Exact oracle (soft-goal value iteration, policy SF tables, GPI) qualified against brute force and against `space.py` h\*; floors (STOP, RAND, GREEDY) separated from the oracle; exact-GPI library ceiling | "The battery can separate SF/GPI from its shortcuts" |
| **Mechanism (this experiment)** | CPU learners (about 180k-parameter MLPs) on exact labels; the frozen R-04 GNN as a baseline | "A small external specialist does or does not transfer to new goal weights" (tier B) |

**External specialist vs internalized.** E05's SF critic is an **external specialist**: an exact successor function,
a learned critic and a GPI argmax outside any LM. Internalization starts only at native-C, behind its own gates.

### 1.5 Prespecified directional predictions (to be copied into the prereg, never back-fitted)

1. **Exact-GPI ceiling.** The ceiling over the 64-policy library is low on H-ID (≤ 0.15) and higher on H-K and H-NEG.
   No library policy pursues 5–6-fact configurations or avoids facts, although step-wise policy switching recovers
   part of this.
2. **SF-GPI vs UVFA.** The SF advantage is largest on H-K and H-NEG (weight extrapolation), and also on secondary H-SPAN
   and H-COST. It is near parity on H-ID, where UVFA interpolates.
3. **Attribution.** UVFA+aux (the scalar critic with ψ as an auxiliary target) closes part of the gap on H-ID only.
4. **Assumption boundary.** On H-CONJ (nonlinear reward) and H-DYN (changed dynamics), SF-GPI degrades to
   ENUM-MIS level or worse. ENUM-H\* and GNN-ENUM do well on H-CONJ.
5. **Confidence.** About 0.55 for P1, about 0.6 for P2, and about 0.35 for an overall ADVANCE. The main risk is the
   strong non-learned guard G1: Blocksworld's subgoal enumeration with a misplaced-blocks bound may already suffice.

## 3. Task definition (exact)

**State space.** Blocksworld with n = 7 under BWM 4-op semantics (`space.successors`, which is verified equal to
`B.step` in `tests/test_bw.py`):
- |S| = 65,990, of which 37,633 are hand-empty and 28,357 are holding states;
- 186,578 move edges, so on average **2.83 legal moves per state, plus STOP**.

The space is name-free: block ids are 0–6. Names (`b0`–`b6`) are attached only for BWM-format calls (exclusions, GNN
encoding).

**Features.** φ(s) ∈ {0,1}^56:
- `on(i,j)` for i ≠ j (42);
- `ontable(i)` (7);
- `clear(i)` (7), as derived by `space.to_facts`. A held block has none of these facts.

**Reward and termination (episodic, undiscounted net benefit).**
- Actions are the legal moves (cost c each) plus **STOP**, which is always legal and collects the terminal utility
  w·φ(s).
- The goal vector is w̄ = (w, −c). Train weights use c = 0.25.
- Weights are small integers, and c ∈ {0.125, 0.25, 0.75} is dyadic, so every value is an exact float64 dyadic
  rational and ties are exact.
- V\*_w(s) = max( w·φ(s), max over legal s′ of [ −c + V\*_w(s′) ] ).
- This is solved by max-plus value iteration over the forward CSR graph, initialized at the STOP values. It converges
  in at most diameter + 1 sweeps, because c > 0 and Blocksworld has no dead ends.

**Policies and successor features.**
- π_w is greedy on V\*_w. Ties are broken deterministically: STOP first, then the lowest action code.
- For a deterministic policy, ψ^π(s) = (φ(s_end^π(s)), T^π(s)): the endpoint's features and the number of moves
  before STOP.
- For a move a to s′: ψ^π(s, a) = (0, 1) + ψ^π(s′). Then Q^π_w(s, a) = w̄·ψ^π(s, a), and V\*_w = w̄·ψ^{π_w} exactly.
- In this deterministic episodic setting, SF is a *policy-conditional endpoint-and-length predictor*. The notation
  stays general, so a γ < 1 or stochastic variant is a parameter change.

**GPI.** For a test weight w′ and a policy set C:

  π_GPI(s) = argmax over {STOP} ∪ moves of max over z ∈ C of Q^{π_z}_{w′}(s, a),   with Q(s, STOP) = w′·φ(s).

With exact ψ, Barreto's theorem gives Q^{π_GPI} ≥ max over z of Q^{π_z}.

**Decision protocol (identical for every arm).**
- At state s, the candidates are STOP plus all legal moves (hard legality comes first, from the exact successor
  function). Each arm scores the candidates and the argmax is taken, with the tie rule above.
- STOP is scored exactly as w′·φ(s) by every arm, because φ is observable. Only the continuation values are learned.
- Closed loop: from start s0, act until STOP, H_max = 40 moves, or a revisited state. A revisit forces a STOP at the
  current state and is logged as a loop.

**Metrics.**
- Return: J = w′·φ(s_final) − c·T.
- **Normalized regret:** nreg(s0, w′) = clip( (V\*_{w′}(s0) − J) / (V\*_{w′}(s0) − w′·φ(s0)), 0, 1 ).
  - It is 0 when optimal, and 1 when the arm does no better than stopping immediately (or worse).
  - Start states must have a gap V\* − w′·φ(s0) ≥ 1.0, that is ≥ 4 steps of cost at c = 0.25. This filter is applied
    at generation time from the oracle and is outcome-free.
- **One-step re-ranking regret** (secondary, the roadmap's "re-ranking"):
  regret1(s) = (max over a of Q\*(s, a) − Q\*(s, â)) / c, in step units, plus the top-1 optimal-action rate. It is
  measured on a fixed state bank, so every arm faces **identical candidate banks**.

### 4.2 How labels are produced (oracle, never LM judgement)

**Per training weight w_i:**
- one value-iteration solve gives V\*_{w_i} over all 65,990 states;
- following π_{w_i} with pointer doubling gives endpoint ids e_i(s) and lengths T_i(s);
- so ψ^{π_{w_i}}(s) = (φ(states[e_i(s)]), T_i(s)), which is exact.

A **label** is one (state, weight) oracle query. The SF arm's target is the 57-vector ψ^{π_{w_i}}(s). The UVFA target
is the scalar V\*_{w_i}(s) = w̄_i·ψ^{π_{w_i}}(s). Both come from the same oracle pass, so their oracle cost is equal. The
richer supervision of SF is disclosed and controlled (UVFA+aux, §5.2).

**Exact policy tables for the library** (int32 endpoints and int16 lengths, 64 × 65,990, about 25 MB) are stored for
the exact-GPI ceiling only. They are an oracle-information reference, *not* a label-matched competitor.

**Modified oracles for the stress families:**
- H-CONJ: terminal reward R·1[G ⊆ s];
- H-DYN: height-limit-3 dynamics, solved by value iteration on the masked edge set.

### 4.5 Leakage and OOD controls

1. **Parent disjointness.** Canonical weighted classes are disjoint across TRAIN, DEV-ID, DEV-OOD and every TEST family
   (asserted).
   - For H-ID, the **unweighted support class** is also recorded. The "support class seen in TRAIN" stratum is reported
     separately, so re-weighted versions of seen fact patterns cannot pass as new directions.
2. **States are shared by design.** Up to renaming, n = 7 has only 26 state orbits (15 hand-empty partitions of 7 plus
   11 holding partitions of 6). Holding out *states* is therefore meaningless. The unit of novelty is the (state,
   weight) pair, which is new because the weight classes are disjoint.
   - Renaming augmentation (a joint random σ on s and w with exactly transformed labels) is applied identically to
     every learned arm.
   - The PGV arms see a single fixed w, so they get no augmentation. That is disclosed.

## 5. Models and baselines

All learned models are CPU MLPs with ≤ 200k parameters, run with `torch.set_num_threads(4)`, and stay under 1 GB RSS.

**Inputs.**
- State x_s (70 dims): the on-matrix (49), ontable (7), holding (7) and clear (7).
- Goal or policy code z (57 dims): fact weights / 3, plus c.

**Shared trunk.** 3 × 256 with LayerNorm and ReLU, AdamW (wd 1e-4), batch 256.

### 5.1 Proposed method

**SF-GPI** (goal-conditioned successor features, the USFA form):
- Network: ψθ(x_s, z) → (56 sigmoid endpoint-fact probabilities, softplus T̂). About 179k parameters.
- Loss: BCE on facts plus Huber on T/10.
- Scoring at test, for each successor s′:
  max over z ∈ C of [ w′·ψ̂_φ(s′, z) − c′·(1 + T̂(s′, z)) ],   with C = L ∪ {w′} (65 codes).

### 5.2 Learned comparators and attribution controls (identical labels, trunk and schedule)

| Arm | Definition | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **UVFA** | V_θ(x_s, w) → scalar V\*; Huber loss; about 164k parameters | **P1 comparator** (bundle E05: "SF vs scalar at equal labels") |
| UVFA+aux | Shared trunk with a scalar head (used at test) and a ψ head (auxiliary, λ = 1) | Card control "consequence features at equal labels". It separates *richer supervision* from *linear recomposition* |
| SF-noGPI | C = {w′} only | Role of GPI |
| SF-L | C = L only (library, no z = w′) | Role of extrapolating ψ to the new policy index |
| SF-GPI-5 | C = {w′} plus the 4 nearest library codes (cosine) | Compute-matched view, about 5 calls vs 1 for UVFA |
| SF-shuffled | SF trained with ψ targets permuted across states within each weight (1 seed) | Card control "shuffled-label critic" |
| **PGV_k** | Per-goal value regression: V_θ(x_s) with no goal input, trained from scratch for **each** held-out primary parent on k uniform labels of that goal, k ∈ {400 (= the per-goal budget of each library goal), 1,600, 6,400}. Uses UVFA's selected hyperparameters, ≥ 2,000 steps, early stopping on 10% of its own k labels | **P2 comparator** (k = 400); label-cost curve |

## 6. Endpoints, statistics and decision rule

### 6.1 Primary endpoint

**The macro-average over H-ID, H-K and H-NEG of the parent-level mean normalized closed-loop regret.**
- Each parent contributes the mean over its 16 fixed starts.
- For learned arms, a parent's value is also averaged over 3 model seeds.
- The data are the 168 fresh TEST parents at n = 7.
- Lower is better.

### 6.2 Primary comparison family (Holm, family-wise α = 0.05)

- **P1 (superiority, equal labels).** D1 = nreg(UVFA) − nreg(SF-GPI).
  - Pass: D1 ≥ 0.05 (the minimum practical effect), the Holm-adjusted two-sided p < 0.05 (CI excludes 0), **and**
    D1 > 0 in each of the 3 seeds.
- **P2 (non-inferiority vs per-goal training at equal per-goal budget).** D2 = nreg(SF-GPI) − nreg(PGV_400).
  - Pass: the Holm-adjusted one-sided upper confidence bound of D2 < δ_NI = 0.05.

### 6.3 Secondary (descriptive, prespecified, no decision weight)

- Per-family results, per-seed results, and the H-ID support-seen and conflict strata.
- One-step re-ranking regret and top-1 optimal rate on the identical bank.
- Loop rate.
- **k\***: the smallest per-goal budget at which PGV matches SF-GPI, by log-k interpolation, with a bootstrap CI.
  It is censored at > 6,400.
- Attribution:
  - SF-GPI vs UVFA+aux (supervision vs recomposition);
  - SF-noGPI and SF-L vs SF-GPI;
  - SF-GPI vs EXACT-GPI(L).
- The compute-matched view (SF-GPI-5).
- GNN-ENUM, ENUM-MIS and ENUM-H\* on every family.
- The H-SPAN, H-COST, H-KN, H-CONJ and H-DYN results, labelled "assumption boundary" where predicted.
- Endpoint-fact accuracy and T MAE of ψ̂, and V MAE of UVFA, on TEST states.

### 6.4 Statistics

- **Unit of independence.** The parent: the canonical weighted class. Starts and one-step states are within-cluster
  repeats, and renamed variants stay within their cluster. **No goal weight, start or seed is counted as independent
  evidence.**
- **P1.** `rcx.stats.seed_parent_bootstrap` (seeds averaged within parent; parents resampled within family, so the
  bootstrap is stratified by family; 10,000 resamples; seed 20261249) and `bootstrap_p_two_sided`. Per-seed parent
  bootstraps are also reported.
- **P2.** A parent bootstrap, with SF pooled over seeds and PGV fitted once per parent (seed 20261248).
- **Holm** over {P1, P2} via `rcx.stats.holm`. Everything else is descriptive and unadjusted.
- **Binary view (secondary).** Per parent, whether the arm is optimal on at least half of the starts, compared with
  exact McNemar. Descriptive only.
- **Attempt log.** Every confirmation attempt, including VOID reruns on a fresh TEST draw, is logged in
  `runs/RCX-E05/ATTEMPTS.jsonl`. A rerun is never called a fresh confirmation of the same bank.

### 6.5 Decision rule (evaluated in this order)

1. **VOID.** An instrument gate fails (App. B checks, floors not separated from the oracle, or an exclusion or
   disjointness assertion). Fix the problem and redraw TEST from the next derived stream, logging the attempt.
2. **ADVANCE.** P1 passes, P2 passes, **and** guard G1 holds: macro nreg(SF-GPI) ≤ macro nreg(ENUM-MIS) + 0.05.
3. **STOP** (for this testbed; the assumption boundary is recorded), if either:
   - (a) the EXACT-GPI(L) ceiling has primary macro nreg ≥ 0.5, so the SF/GPI mechanism does not fit Blocksworld soft
     goals; or
   - (b) P1 is futile (the upper CI bound of D1 < 0.05) **and** P2 fails.
4. **REVISE.** P1 or P2 passes but not ADVANCE. This includes "both pass but a non-learned enumeration planner is as
   good" (G1 fails), and "only the label-cost claim holds".
5. **INCONCLUSIVE.** Otherwise. A positive point estimate with a wide interval is not a pass, and a nonsignificant loss
   is not equivalence.

**Attribution qualifier (stated with any outcome).** Suppose UVFA+aux is within 0.02 of SF-GPI. Then the gain is
credited to vector consequence supervision, not to test-time linear recomposition, and the claim wording changes
accordingly.

### 6.6 Power estimate

**Assumptions.**
- The parent-level SD of the paired difference is σ ∈ [0.15, 0.25]. The difference is bounded in [−1, 1], and each
  parent averages 16 starts and 3 seeds.
- The test is two-sided at the Holm first-step α = 0.025, so z = 2.24.
- The effect is MPE = 0.05.

**Power at n = 168 parents (normal approximation).**

| σ | Power |
|---|---|
| 0.15 | 0.98 |
| 0.20 | **0.84** |
| 0.25 | 0.64 |

P2's non-inferiority power at a true difference of 0 is the same.

**Re-sizing rule (frozen in the prereg, fixed before TEST is drawn).**
- The DEV pilot estimates σ̂ from DEV-ID plus DEV-OOD parents (72).
- If σ̂ > 0.22, the size rises to 72 parents per family (216; power ≈ 0.76 at σ = 0.25).
- The size is capped at 80 per family (240) by the compute budget.
