# Item 7: seed replication of the frozen B2 system (the release gate)

Written 2026-10-10 ~15:00 CDT, before any seed adapter is trained and before the fresh set has been evaluated by
any system. The user approved this design on 2026-10-10 (D-65, decision 4).

## Question

Does the B2 result (world model + search far above the same proposer alone) depend on the particular training seeds
of the two adapters? Every frozen result so far uses one seed per adapter: F = `BW_Fdelta_s0`, Pa = `BW_Pa_v2_s1`.

## Systems (frozen B2 configurations; only the adapter paths change)

- **R0 (anchor):** F = `runs/BW_Fdelta_s0`, Pa = `runs/BW_Pa_v2_s1` (the frozen B2 adapters).
- **R1:** F = `runs/BW_Fdelta_s1`, Pa = `runs/BW_Pa_v2_s2`.
- **R2:** F = `runs/BW_Fdelta_s2`, Pa = `runs/BW_Pa_v2_s3`.

**Training recipes** (`scripts/train_generic.py`; the seed sets both the LoRA initialisation and the data order):

| Adapter | Rows | Steps | Batch | lr | Max-len | Seeds |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| F | `runs/BW_data/forward_delta_train.jsonl` | 600 | 16 | 1e-4 | 256 | 1, 2 |
| Pa | `runs/BW_data_v2/proposer_actions_train.jsonl` | 1000 | 16 | 1e-4 | 256 | 2, 3 |

These are the exact recipes of `BW_Fdelta_s0` and `BW_Pa_v2_s1`, per their `metrics.json`.

**Per system, two configurations:**
- **S-beam:** `FREEZE_B2_beam` args (K 8, W 4, E 24, R 12, M 80, P 1, delta F, eval_seed 0).
- **S-direct:** `FREEZE_B2_direct` args (greedy, K 1, M 256, eval_seed 0).

The freeze records `runs/FREEZE_I7_{R0,R1,R2}_{beam,direct}.json` hold every adapter SHA-256. They are written after
training and before any evaluation.

## Fresh test set

- `runs/BW_val_large/instances_seeds_fresh.json`: n = 40, generated by the mirror generator.
- Block counts are the hard set's distribution, stratified (every 110/40-th of its sorted sizes).
- Seed 20261014.
- Excludes:
  - hard-set renaming signatures;
  - the invariants of hard, val_large, mirror, b3fresh and the 240 distillation problems.
- Generated and hashed now. No system has been run on it.

## Registered tests

- **Per replicate R1 and R2: "the world-model effect replicates"** iff both hold:
  - S-beam beats S-direct on the 40 instances, paired exact McNemar p < 0.01;
  - S-beam − S-direct ≥ +0.30.
- **Release-gate reading: "the B2 effect replicates across seeds"** iff both R1 and R2 replicate.
- **Report-only:**
  - success per system and configuration, with Clopper-Pearson 95% CIs;
  - R1 and R2 vs R0, paired McNemar per configuration. Seed spread is reported; no equality claim is made, and a
    non-significant difference is not read as equivalence;
  - success by block count;
  - F queries and proposer tokens per instance;
  - VAL agreement.

## Not claimed

- Any change to the frozen B2 hard, mirror or 600 numbers. Those stay single-seed results run once.
- Seed robustness of B3's Pa_v3 or of D-54's FK.
