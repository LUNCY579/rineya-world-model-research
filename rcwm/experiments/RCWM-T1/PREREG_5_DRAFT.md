## 1. Question

PREREG-3 found that T2 s0's ledger no-op is the token 'void' (STATUS: TRIGGER; unseen statuses 0.01-0.18) and that it fails out of range on magnitudes, reordered keys and word account names; PREREG-LB found that T2 s0 plans at 0.706 on its training surface but 5/180 on the held-out combined surface. T2b-ledger trains the same recipe (D-15/D-16: 1,500 oracle-labelled ledger one-step rows, LoRA r16, 190 steps, the vendored BWM trainer) on a SURFACE-DIVERSIFIED set whose training and held-out partitions were fixed at build time (`docs/t2b/ledger/DESIGN.md` section 2). The question, in the shared wording agreed with BWM (D-54):

- **(a)** For each surface factor X (status words, key order, account names, account count, rule paraphrase, magnitude, and all of them combined), does T2b-ledger **generalise to unseen X** (on held-out items never in training: the absolute bar, non-inferiority to its own canonical accuracy, and a significant gain over the unaugmented T2 s0), or is the **trigger widened** (it improved only on the trained variants and stays near T2 s0 on held-out ones), or is it **partial**?
- **(b)** Used as the world model inside BWM's beam planner, does T2b-ledger **plan on the unseen surface** (PREREG-LB's held-out combined rendering), where T2 s0 solved 5/180?
- **(c)** Does T2b-ledger seed 0 retain Titan-Sparse's general competence on the 5c guard?

## 5. Decision rules (registered verbatim; applied mechanically by `scripts/t2bl_confirm_analysis.py`)

### 5.1 Part (a), the one-step map

> PREREG-5 part (a), the one-step map. Bank: the sealed map bank minted at the freeze (t2bl_common.CONFIRM_STRATA: 28 strata on 100 fresh bases, rendered with the CONFIRM half of every split held-out partition, which the dev screen never used; S_bal, S_amt and H_amt on the 67 add / transfer bases). Sides, all generated in one process: OFF (Titan-Sparse, adapters disabled), T2 s0 (PREREG-2's adapter) and T2b-ledger seeds 0, 1, 2, unmerged. Scoring: p3_common.score_onestep (exact balances, any key order). For every primary cell c (words: H_words; key order: H_order; names: H_names; count: H_count; paraphrase: H_para; magnitude: H_bal on the add / transfer bases and H_amt; combined held-out surface: HS_words and HS_posted; H_order, H_names, H_count and H_para carry posted events on even bases and the base's training non-posted word on odd bases, the magnitude cells posted events only) and every T2b-ledger seed s: BAR(c, s) iff accuracy >= 0.80 and Wilson 95% lower bound >= 0.70 (on the 67-row cells that is at least 55 of 67); GAIN(c, s) iff the exact two-sided McNemar test of seed s against T2 s0 on c's rows is significant at 0.05 after Holm adjustment over the 27 (cell, seed) tests, with seed s ahead (b > c); NI(c) iff every seed's difference between its accuracy on c and its accuracy on the same bases' canonical twins (ID_posted for posted rows, ID_void for the held-out-word rows, S_words for the training-word rows) is >= -0.10 and the paired base-bootstrap one-sided 95% lower bound of that difference averaged over the three seeds (bases resampled with all their rows and seeds; 10,000 resamples, seed 17) is >= -0.10. Per factor X: GENERALISES TO UNSEEN X iff BAR and GAIN hold for every cell of X in all three seeds and NI holds for every cell of X; TRIGGER WIDENED iff the factor's trained-variant control (words: S_words; magnitude: S_bal and S_amt; every other factor: S_surface_posted and S_surface_words; all on in-range values) has, in all three seeds, accuracy >= 0.80 and at least 0.10 above T2 s0's on the same rows, while, on every cell of X and in every seed, the seed's accuracy is within 0.10 of T2 s0's (above or below) and at least one cell of X misses BAR in every seed; PARTIAL otherwise, reported with the per-cell, per-variant, per-seed table and its named patterns (below_bar, not_noninferior, no_gain, no_headroom where T2 s0 itself meets BAR on a cell without GAIN, below_t2s0 where a seed is more than 0.10 below T2 s0 on a cell, seed_disagreement). In-distribution control: ID_NI iff the paired base-bootstrap one-sided 95% lower bound of T2b-ledger's accuracy minus T2 s0's on ID_posted and ID_void (200 rows), averaged over the three seeds, is >= -0.05 (each seed's difference is reported); if ID_NI fails, every part (a) headline carries 'at an in-distribution cost relative to T2 s0'.

Factor labels used in the wording: words: 'unseen status words'; order: 'unseen key orders'; names: 'unseen account names'; count: 'an unseen account count (8 accounts)'; para: 'an unseen rule paraphrase'; magnitude: 'unseen magnitudes (posted add / transfer events: 5-digit balances, 4-digit amounts)'; combined: 'the combined unseen surface'.

### 5.2 Part (a), secondary: PREREG-3's 16 strings

> PREREG-5 part (a), secondary (comparable with PREREG-3's V1; never changes a factor verdict): PREREG-3's V1 rule applied to each T2b-ledger seed on this bank's eight PREREG-3 strata (U_pending, U_draft, U_cancelled, U_reversed, U_held, U_neutral, N_voidcase, N_postedlike: PREREG-3's 16 non-posted strings, none of them in T2b-ledger's training), with ID_posted as the posted twins and ID_void as C_void: RULE iff in every seed every one of the eight strata meets BAR and the twin guard holds (ID_posted accuracy >= 0.80 and copied <= 0.10); TRIGGER iff in every seed the seven strata without a form of 'void' are each <= 0.30, ID_void >= 0.80 and more than half of the errors on those seven strata apply the event as if posted; PARTIAL otherwise. The same rule is applied to T2 s0 in this run as the comparator. Gains over T2 s0 per stratum and seed: exact two-sided McNemar, Holm over the 24 at 0.05.

### 5.3 Part (b), planning on the unseen surface

> PREREG-5 part (b), planning. Instances: the sealed planning draw minted at the freeze (lbeam_problems generator v2, 36 per optimal depth 2-6, N = 180, in range and closed under the vocabulary). Planner: BWM's plan_beam (native_v1/bwmn/blocks_planner.py, sha256 18a5f22b94e5304f9dfe117d3afa7d89e5f21596b86ec0412b25d0b24b9ab960) with lbeam_proposer.EnumerateProposer at width 2 and 12 expansions (lbeam_proposer.beam_kwargs), eval_seed 0, every F wrapped in lbeam_f.guarded (the registered range guard). Arms (one GPU stage each; every turn loads one PeftModel holding both adapters unmerged and re-runs A0 before any stage): T2s0@HS = lbeam_f.SurfaceAuditF('T2', 'heldout_combined') with adapter 't2' (PREREG-LB's held-out combined rendering: keys reversed, accounts bank / card / cash / fees, 'void' rendered 'cancelled'); T2BL@HS = the same with T2b-ledger seed 0's adapter; T2s0@canon and T2BL@canon = lbeam_f.LedgerF('T2') with each adapter; ORACLE = exact env.step (CPU only). Success: the one submitted plan reaches the goal exactly under env.step. PRIMARY: T2BL@HS vs T2s0@HS paired over all 180 instances, exact two-sided McNemar on the discordant pairs (b = T2BL only, c = T2s0 only), delta = (b - c) / 180: PASS iff p < 0.01 and delta >= +0.20; SHORT iff p < 0.01, b > c and delta < +0.20; REVERSED iff p < 0.01 and c > b; NS otherwise. Reading: GENERALISES (T2b-ledger plans on the unseen surface) iff PASS, the absolute bar success(T2BL@HS) / success(ORACLE) >= 0.70 holds, and T2BL@HS is non-inferior to T2BL@canon (paired instance-bootstrap one-sided 95% lower bound of success(T2BL@HS) - success(T2BL@canon) >= -0.10; 10,000 resamples, seed 17); TRIGGER WIDENED iff success(T2BL@canon) - success(T2s0@canon) >= -0.05 and the primary is NS with delta within 0.10 of zero; PARTIAL otherwise, with its named reason (short, below_bar, not_noninferior, reversed, canonical_cost, ns_with_gain). Report-only: NEAR-ORACLE on the unseen surface iff the T2BL@HS / ORACLE ratio is >= 0.80 with paired bootstrap 95% lower bound >= 0.70; T2BL@canon vs T2s0@canon (exact McNemar, the difference with its interval); success by optimal depth and on the needs-lookahead stratum, verified-plan precision, refused keys and void-twin exposure for every arm; IGNORE_STATUS (CPU).

### 5.4 Part (c), the retention guard

> PREREG-5 part (c), the retention guard: the frozen 5c retention suite (docs/retention/RET_FREEZE.json) on a confirmation draw minted at run time (ret_gen.py mint --purpose confirm: a fresh OS-entropy seed, the registered dev selection), run by ret_run.py --mode confirm with T2b-ledger seed 0 as the extra Titan-Sparse adapter 'x_t2bl_s0' and scored once by ret_analysis.py analyze under its registered RULE_CONFIRM. The guard is the comparison 'x_t2bl_s0_vs_off': GO (k/m binding) iff RETAINED; NO-GO iff LOSS DETECTED or MARGIN EXCEEDED. It is reported with every headline and never changes a part (a) or (b) verdict; a NO-GO adds 'T2b-ledger s0 loses retention against Titan-Sparse on the 5c guard; it is not a packaging candidate without retention repair'. If the retention run is not completed, the guard is 'not evaluable' and every headline says so.

### 5.5 How the readings map to the shared wording (D-54)

- "generalises to unseen X" is read exactly when GENERALISES holds for X: on held-out surfaces never in training (and never in the dev screen), the absolute bar (0.80, Wilson lower bound 0.70), non-inferiority to its own canonical accuracy (margin 0.10), and a Holm-significant gain over T2 s0, in all three seeds. Where T2 s0 itself already meets the bar on a cell (no headroom), a gain cannot be shown and the factor is PARTIAL with the 'no shown gain where T2 s0 already meets the bar' clause: the joint definition requires a significant gain over the unaugmented arm. The factor cells for order, names, count and paraphrase mix posted rows with TRAINING non-posted words, so T2 s0 (which knows only 'void') has headroom on all of them, including the 8-account count.
- "trigger widened" is read exactly when TRIGGER WIDENED holds: the trained variants pass (>= 0.80) and improved on T2 s0 (by at least 0.10), while every held-out cell stays within 0.10 of T2 s0 (above or below) with a cell below the bar in every seed. A seed far below T2 s0 is not 'near' it: that is PARTIAL with the below_t2s0 pattern.
- "partial" otherwise, always with the per-cell, per-variant, per-seed table.
- Part (b) uses the same three words for planning (GENERALISES = plans on the unseen surface).

**Why these thresholds.** 0.80 with a Wilson lower bound of 0.70 is the programme's bar (PREREG-3); on the 67-row magnitude cells the Wilson bound makes it 55 of 67 (0.82), not 54. The own-canonical margin 0.10 and the in-distribution margin 0.05 (against T2 s0) follow the task's dev gate; both are tested on the seed mean with a paired base bootstrap (each seed's own difference must also clear the cell margin), which is what makes them testable at 100 bases (section 5b). Because a no-op row's twin is ID_void (about 0.99 for an adapter that learned the canonical frame), the own-canonical clause asks a held-out-word cell for about 0.93 or more in practice. Holm at 0.05 over the 27 (cell, seed) gain tests controls the family of 'significantly above T2 s0' claims. The control gain of 0.10 makes "improved on the trained variants" a stated quantity. Planning keeps PREREG-LB's +0.20 at p < 0.01 for its primary; the absolute bar is 0.70 of ORACLE's success (T2 s0 reached 0.794 of ORACLE on its own surface in PREREG-LB), and the own-canonical margin 0.10 mirrors part (a). These values were set with knowledge of PREREG-3's and PREREG-LB's aggregate results (stated in the limits), never of a T2b-ledger output.

- Retention: a retention run that stops (persisted) is bound as stopped and part (c) reads 'not evaluable'; parts (a) and (b) are unaffected.
- Analysis crash before the results exist: an identical rerun is allowed (an orphan PREREG_5_RESULTS.md without the JSON is moved aside); a crash fix only as `PREREG_5_AMENDMENT_*.json` (file, old and new sha256, reason, `approved_by`), logged.

## Appendix A. Choices made in this draft, with reasons

- **The dev gate precedes the freeze.** A sealed confirmation is drafted from a kit whose dev screen passed a pre-stated gate (rule revision 2, unchanged at kit revision 3: held-out words >= 0.80 on the combined held-out surface and on the canonical surface, every word kind >= 0.60, the held-out posted twins >= 0.80 correct and copied <= 0.10, held-out words within 0.10 of the seed's own canonical no-op accuracy, in distribution within 0.05 of T2 s0, in all seeds). The gate decides only whether PREREG-5 runs; its clauses mirror this test's, so a PROCEED is not almost sure to read PARTIAL here (section 5b).
- **Held-out halves.** Every held-out partition the gate reads (words, names, key orders, paraphrase) is split before training into a DEV half and a CONFIRM half; this test reads only the confirm half, so it does not re-test the items the gate conditioned on. Not split: the 8-account count and the magnitude bands (the gate reads neither), PREREG-LB's rendering (fixed by PREREG-LB). The top-level reversal (the only other order of a 2-key object) is no longer held out: it is trained since kit revision 3, so the top-level key order is held out nowhere (no bank has an untrained top-level order) and every key-order claim covers the inner orders of the event and before objects only (a registered limit).
- **Token-novel held-out words.** A held-out spelling variant built from the 'void' or 'posted' token (voidd = [void, d]) is near-trained for a token model; every held-out word avoids both anchor tokens and has a token no trained status has, so a trigger widened to the trained anchors scores 0 on them.
- **Mixed factor cells.** H_order, H_names, H_count and H_para carry posted events on even bases and the base's training word on odd bases (twin S_words), so apply-regardless scores 0.50 and the status is read under each unseen factor; a training word, not 'void', keeps T2 s0's own cue from handing it half of every cell.
- **In-range trained-variant controls, and magnitude controls.** The controls of the surface factors keep the base's in-range values, so 'trigger widened' is not blocked by arithmetic; the magnitude factor has its own controls in the trained bands (S_bal, S_amt).
- **Seed 0 is the registered artifact** for planning and retention (fixed before training); seeds 1 and 2 replicate the map.
- **Non-inferiority on the seed mean.** Per-seed one-sided intervals at 100 bases would fail a true non-inferior adapter most of the time (section 5b); the seed mean with a paired base bootstrap keeps the interval honest while every seed's own difference must also clear the margin.
