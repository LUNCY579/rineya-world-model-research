## 1. Question

PREREG-3 found that T2 s0's ledger no-op is the token 'void' (STATUS: TRIGGER; unseen statuses 0.01-0.18) and that it fails out of range on magnitudes, reordered keys and word account names; PREREG-LB found that T2 s0 plans at 0.706 on its training surface but 5/180 on the held-out combined surface. T2b-ledger trains the same recipe (D-15/D-16: 1,500 oracle-labelled ledger one-step rows, LoRA r16, 190 steps, the vendored BWM trainer) on a SURFACE-DIVERSIFIED set whose training and held-out partitions were fixed at build time (`docs/t2b/ledger/DESIGN.md` section 2). The question, in the shared wording agreed with BWM (D-54):

- **(a)** For each surface factor X (status words, key order, account names, account count, rule paraphrase, magnitude, and all of them combined), does T2b-ledger **generalise to unseen X** (on held-out items never in training: the absolute bar, non-inferiority to its own canonical accuracy, and a significant gain over the unaugmented T2 s0), or is the **trigger widened** (it improved only on the trained variants and stays near T2 s0 on held-out ones), or is it **partial**?
- **(b)** Used as the world model inside BWM's beam planner, does T2b-ledger **plan on the unseen surface** (PREREG-LB's held-out combined rendering), where T2 s0 solved 5/180?
- **(c)** Does T2b-ledger seed 0 retain Titan-Sparse's general competence on the 5c guard?

### 5.5 How the readings map to the shared wording (D-54)

- "generalises to unseen X" is read exactly when GENERALISES holds for X: on held-out surfaces never in training (and never in the dev screen), the absolute bar (0.80, Wilson lower bound 0.70), non-inferiority to its own canonical accuracy (margin 0.10), and a Holm-significant gain over T2 s0, in all three seeds. Where T2 s0 itself already meets the bar on a cell (no headroom), a gain cannot be shown and the factor is PARTIAL with the 'no shown gain where T2 s0 already meets the bar' clause: the joint definition requires a significant gain over the unaugmented arm. The factor cells for order, names, count and paraphrase mix posted rows with TRAINING non-posted words, so T2 s0 (which knows only 'void') has headroom on all of them, including the 8-account count.
- "trigger widened" is read exactly when TRIGGER WIDENED holds: the trained variants pass (>= 0.80) and improved on T2 s0 (by at least 0.10), while every held-out cell stays within 0.10 of T2 s0 (above or below) with a cell below the bar in every seed. A seed far below T2 s0 is not 'near' it: that is PARTIAL with the below_t2s0 pattern.
- "partial" otherwise, always with the per-cell, per-variant, per-seed table.
- Part (b) uses the same three words for planning (GENERALISES = plans on the unseen surface).

**Why these thresholds.** 0.80 with a Wilson lower bound of 0.70 is the programme's bar (PREREG-3); on the 67-row magnitude cells the Wilson bound makes it 55 of 67 (0.82), not 54. The own-canonical margin 0.10 and the in-distribution margin 0.05 (against T2 s0) follow the task's dev gate; both are tested on the seed mean with a paired base bootstrap (each seed's own difference must also clear the cell margin), which is what makes them testable at 100 bases (section 5b). Because a no-op row's twin is ID_void (about 0.99 for an adapter that learned the canonical frame), the own-canonical clause asks a held-out-word cell for about 0.93 or more in practice. Holm at 0.05 over the 27 (cell, seed) gain tests controls the family of 'significantly above T2 s0' claims. The control gain of 0.10 makes "improved on the trained variants" a stated quantity. Planning keeps PREREG-LB's +0.20 at p < 0.01 for its primary; the absolute bar is 0.70 of ORACLE's success (T2 s0 reached 0.794 of ORACLE on its own surface in PREREG-LB), and the own-canonical margin 0.10 mirrors part (a). These values were set with knowledge of PREREG-3's and PREREG-LB's aggregate results (stated in the limits), never of a T2b-ledger output.

**Deviation and stop policy.**
- A0 failure in the first A0 (anchor, adapter identity or batch invariance): stop before any output (persisted); no verdict; investigate and log; a software or environment cause is restored and the stop records moved aside with a DECISIONS entry; any other remedy is an amendment before any output exists.
- A0 re-check failure on a later turn: stop before any stage of that turn; parts whose stages were all recorded under a passing A0 stay verdict-eligible (STOPPED.json `eligible_parts`); the other part is rendered 'not evaluable'.
- Any other deterministic stop (runtime drift, fingerprint, adapter hash, BWM planner sha, a changed recorded output, a failed freeze verification, two consecutive turns without a recorded stage, a stage failing three times): persisted; per-part eligibility as above (a changed output makes nothing eligible). A stage attempt FAILS when it ends in a recorded exception or when its process dies without one while the machine keeps running; an attempt cut off by a reboot (an earlier boot time, no exception) is logged as interrupted and does not count.
- GPU crash or reset: a stage that is not done is moved to `runs/prereg5/_orphans/` (never deleted) and regenerated in full; the resumed turn re-runs A0 first. A stale `.partial` of a single-writer record (DONE.json, RET_DRAW.json, RETENTION.json, the results) is moved aside, never deleted, before the record is written.
- Retention: a retention run that stops (persisted) is bound as stopped and part (c) reads 'not evaluable'; parts (a) and (b) are unaffected.
- Analysis crash before the results exist: an identical rerun is allowed (an orphan PREREG_5_RESULTS.md without the JSON is moved aside); a crash fix only as `PREREG_5_AMENDMENT_*.json` (file, old and new sha256, reason, `approved_by`), logged.

## Appendix A. Choices made in this draft, with reasons

- **The dev gate precedes the freeze.** A sealed confirmation is drafted from a kit whose dev screen passed a pre-stated gate (revision 2: held-out words >= 0.80 on the combined held-out surface and on the canonical surface, every word kind >= 0.60, the held-out posted twins >= 0.80 correct and copied <= 0.10, held-out words within 0.10 of the seed's own canonical no-op accuracy, in distribution within 0.05 of T2 s0, in all seeds). The gate decides only whether PREREG-5 runs; its clauses mirror this test's, so a PROCEED is not almost sure to read PARTIAL here (section 5b).
- **Held-out halves.** Every held-out partition the gate reads (words, names, key orders, paraphrase) is split before training into a DEV half and a CONFIRM half; this test reads only the confirm half, so it does not re-test the items the gate conditioned on. Not split: the top-level reversal (the only other order of a 2-key object), the 8-account count and the magnitude bands (the gate reads neither), PREREG-LB's rendering (fixed by PREREG-LB).
- **Token-novel held-out words.** A held-out spelling variant built from the 'void' or 'posted' token (voidd = [void, d]) is near-trained for a token model; every held-out word avoids both anchor tokens and has a token no trained status has, so a trigger widened to the trained anchors scores 0 on them.
- **Mixed factor cells.** H_order, H_names, H_count and H_para carry posted events on even bases and the base's training word on odd bases (twin S_words), so apply-regardless scores 0.50 and the status is read under each unseen factor; a training word, not 'void', keeps T2 s0's own cue from handing it half of every cell.
- **In-range trained-variant controls, and magnitude controls.** The controls of the surface factors keep the base's in-range values, so 'trigger widened' is not blocked by arithmetic; the magnitude factor has its own controls in the trained bands (S_bal, S_amt).
- **Seed 0 is the registered artifact** for planning and retention (fixed before training); seeds 1 and 2 replicate the map.
- **Non-inferiority on the seed mean.** Per-seed one-sided intervals at 100 bases would fail a true non-inferior adapter most of the time (section 5b); the seed mean with a paired base bootstrap keeps the interval honest while every seed's own difference must also clear the margin.
- **One planning arm per stage, bounded by the ceiling.** A planning stage starts only if its ceiling-size run at the measured rate fits the turn, so no turn overruns 4.5 h even at the ceiling (every simulated schedule in section 7, including 2.5 generations per second, keeps every turn under 4.0 h).
- **The held-out rendering of part (b) is PREREG-LB's**, so 5/180 is the comparable number; it is not one of T2b-ledger's own held-out surfaces (its 'cancelled' is one of PREREG-3's reserved strings, its names are reserved, its key orders are held out).
- **Retention binding by pointer.** The 5c suite is frozen separately and its analysis writes its own create-only result; PREREG-5 binds that result (or the stop) after checking the draw and the adapter, and never re-scores it.
