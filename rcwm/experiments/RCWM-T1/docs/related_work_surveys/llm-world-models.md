## Related work for RCWM-T1: LLMs as world models and simulators for planning

I located every paper below on arXiv or the venue's proceedings page on 2026-10-06. A paper marked [abstract-only] was checked from its abstract alone. A venue marked UNVERIFIED is one I did not confirm.

### (a) Chaining one-step predictions vs. predicting several steps at once (finding 1)

- **Nye et al., 2021. "Show Your Work: Scratchpads for Intermediate Computation with Language Models."** arXiv:2112.00114. https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.00114. Having the model write out intermediate steps greatly improves multi-step computation, such as long addition and program execution, compared with answering directly. RCWM-T1's self-rollout is a scratchpad written outside the model, and the 3-17x recovery fits this result.
- **Li, Liu, Zhou, Ma, 2024. "Chain of Thought Empowers Transformers to Solve Inherently Serial Problems."** ICLR 2024, arXiv:2402.12875. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12875. This is a theory paper. Without chain-of-thought (CoT), constant-depth transformers cannot do inherently serial computation, and T CoT steps add it. In their experiments, CoT gives large gains on permutation composition, especially for shallow models. This predicts that a 0.6B model fails at k-step prediction in one pass but succeeds when the steps are made serial.
- **Merrill, Petty, Sabharwal, 2024. "The Illusion of State in State-Space Models."** ICML 2024, arXiv:2404.08819. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08819. Transformers and state-space models (SSMs) both sit inside the complexity class TC⁰. Neither can solve state tracking such as permutation composition in a single pass. This gives a principled reason to expect k=4 single-pass accuracy near 0.
- **Li, Guo, Andreas, 2025. "(How) Do Language Models Track State?"** ICML 2025, arXiv:2503.02854. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02854. Language models trained on permutation composition learn one of two mechanisms: an associative scan, or a parity-based heuristic. The two differ in robustness, and intermediate training tasks steer which one appears. Training on one-step transitions alone may never produce a mechanism for composing steps.
- **Balesni, Korbak, Evans, 2024/2025. "The Two-Hop Curse" (v1), retitled "Lessons from Studying Two-Hop Latent Reasoning" (v4).** arXiv:2411.16353. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16353. They fine-tune Llama-3-8B and GPT-4o on facts that appear in separate documents. The models cannot combine two such facts without CoT, but can with CoT. This is the closest analogue to RCWM-T1's one-step training followed by collapse at k=2. Version 4 softens the claim: composition works when one of the two facts is a natural, pre-existing one.
- **Dziri et al., 2023. "Faith and Fate: Limits of Transformers on Compositionality."** NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2305.18654. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18654. Transformers handle compositional tasks by "linearized subgraph matching" and do not learn a systematic procedure. Their theoretical argument says accuracy falls quickly as complexity rises. This fits a decay steeper than independent per-step errors would predict.
- **Venkatraman, Hebert, Bagnell, 2015. "Improving Multi-Step Prediction of Learned Time Series Models" (DaD).** AAAI 2015. https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/9590. When a one-step model is chained, its own errors change the inputs it sees at later steps. They fix this by training on the model's own rollouts, an imitation-learning approach. This is a candidate fix for drift in self-rollout.

### (b) Decision quality from LM simulators (findings 2 and 4)

- **Hao et al., 2023. "Reasoning with Language Model is Planning with World Model" (RAP).** arXiv:2305.14992 (venue EMNLP 2023, UNVERIFIED). https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14992. One prompted LLM acts as both world model and agent inside Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS). RAP on LLaMA-33B beats GPT-4 with CoT by 33% relative on plan generation.
- **Zhao, Lee, Hsu, 2023. "Large Language Models as Commonsense Knowledge for Large-Scale Task Planning" (LLM-MCTS).** NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2305.14078. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14078. Using the LLM's world model as the prior for MCTS beats using the LLM as a policy. They propose a minimum-description-length (MDL) rule: use the LLM as a world model when the world model is much simpler to describe than the policy. This matches finding 2, where rollout plus a readout beats direct evaluators.
- **Chae et al., 2025. "Web Agents with World Models" (WMA).** ICLR 2025, arXiv:2410.13232. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13232. A fine-tuned Llama-3.1-8B world model plus an 8B value function picks actions by looking one step ahead. Predicting only the state change gets 22.0% success, against 14.5% for predicting the full observation. Simulating 3 steps instead of 1 gives a small gain (17.1% → 19.6%), but spending the same budget on more candidate actions does slightly better.
- **Gu et al., 2025. "Is Your LLM Secretly a World Model of the Internet?" (WebDreamer).** TMLR 2025, arXiv:2411.06559. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06559. The default plan looks 1 step ahead. Looking 2-3 steps ahead helps less because errors accumulate and simulated trajectories "become less distinguishable." Dreamer-7B (built on Qwen2-VL-7B, trained on 3.1M examples) matches GPT-4o. By contrast, RCWM-T1 gets gains at 4 steps from 384 examples, in a structured domain.
- **Wang et al., 2024. "Can Language Models Serve as Text-Based World Simulators?"** ACL 2024, arXiv:2406.06485. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06485. On their ByteSized32-State-Prediction benchmark, GPT-4 at best gets 77.1% on transitions caused by actions and 49.7% on transitions caused by the environment. Predicting state differences helps for static states and hurts for dynamic ones. They note that 0.599¹⁰ is below 1% after 10 steps. This independent-error baseline is the one RCWM-T1 decays faster than.
- **Yang et al., 2024. "Evaluating World Models with LLM for Decision Making."** arXiv:2411.08794. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08794. They test policy verification, action proposal and planning across 31 environments. GPT-4o is much better than GPT-4o-mini, performance drops on long-horizon tasks, and combining world-model functions adds instability.
- **Li et al., 2025. "From Word to World: Can LLMs be Implicit Text-based World Models?"** arXiv:2512.18832 [abstract-only]. https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18832. Fine-tuned LLM world models in 5 text environments keep a coherent state and scale with data and model size. They help agents through action verification, synthetic trajectories and warm-starting RL. The gains depend on how well the data covers agent behaviour and on environment complexity.
- **Tang, Key, Ellis, 2024. "WorldCoder."** NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2402.12275. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12275. The world model is written as a Python program. It is more sample-efficient than deep RL and transfers to new environments by editing the code. This is the program-based counterpart to RCWM-T1's symbolic baseline that wins with 4000 labels.
- **Vafa et al., 2024. "Evaluating the World Model Implicit in a Generative Model."** NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2406.03689. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03689. Models pass the usual next-token and probe checks, but new metrics based on the Myhill-Nerode theorem show their world models are incoherent and fragile on related tasks.
- **Barreto et al., 2017. "Successor Features for Transfer in Reinforcement Learning."** NeurIPS 2017, arXiv:1606.05312. https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05312. Separating dynamics from rewards, plus generalized policy improvement, gives guaranteed transfer when the reward weights change. This is the theoretical frame for finding 4's advantage on out-of-span goals.

### (c) Small models

- **Xiang et al., 2023. "Language Models Meet World Models."** arXiv:2305.10626 (venue NeurIPS 2023, UNVERIFIED). https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10626. They fine-tune 1.3B, 6B and 13B models on simulator (VirtualHome) experience, using EWC plus LoRA. Average gain is +64.28% on 18 tasks, and the small models match or beat ChatGPT.
- **Handa et al., 2025. "ActionReasoningBench."** ICLR 2025, arXiv:2406.04046. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04046. Accuracy falls slowly as action sequences grow from 1 to 19 steps: GPT-4o goes from 81.3% to 72.3%, and Llama-3.1-8B from 66.4% to 56.6%. A fine-tuned 8B model gains 33.7% and beats GPT-4o. These are mostly binary questions, so the decay is much flatter than RCWM-T1's exact-match collapse.
- **Qiao et al., 2024. "Agent Planning with World Knowledge Model."** NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2405.14205. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14205. A world knowledge model built on 7-8B models improves planning, and a weak world knowledge model can guide a stronger agent model.
- **Lee et al., 2024. "Teaching Arithmetic to Small Transformers."** ICLR 2024. https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/6bf82fdcbd92b6a7793b3894422d2437-Abstract-Conference.html. Training data that includes intermediate steps (CoT format) improves accuracy, sample efficiency and convergence speed, even without pretraining. This suggests an ablation for RCWM-T1: train on multi-step data in that format.

### Probing and mechanism (findings 3 and 5)

- **Li, Nye, Andreas, 2021. "Implicit Representations of Meaning in Neural Language Models."** ACL 2021, arXiv:2106.00737. https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.143. Entity state in BART and T5 can be read out linearly, and editing it changes generation in predictable ways.
- **Kim & Schuster, 2023. "Entity Tracking in Language Models."** ACL 2023, arXiv:2305.02363. https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.213. On their boxes task, among the models tested only the code-pretrained GPT-3.5 models track entities.
- **Hewitt & Liang, 2019. "Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks."** arXiv:1909.03368 (venue EMNLP 2019, UNVERIFIED). https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03368. This paper defines control tasks and selectivity. RCWM-T1's selectivity of about 0 means the probe can compute the consequence from surface features by itself.
- **Prakash et al., 2024. "Fine-Tuning Enhances Existing Mechanisms."** ICLR 2024, arXiv:2402.14811. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14811. Fine-tuned models reuse the base model's entity-tracking circuit, and the gain comes from enhancing it rather than creating something new. This fits a tiny weight change acting as a "mode" offset.
- **Todd et al., 2024. "Function Vectors in Large Language Models."** ICLR 2024, arXiv:2310.15213. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15213. A compact vector, mostly in the middle layers, makes the model carry out a task in new contexts. This is a template for the pending mode-vector steering test.

### Gaps

- I found no paper that fine-tunes a model under 1B parameters on a few hundred one-step examples and then compares single-pass multi-step prediction, self-rollout and hidden-state evaluators on goal-conditioned selection. The search was not exhaustive.
- I looked for no literature on finding 6, the operational issue.
