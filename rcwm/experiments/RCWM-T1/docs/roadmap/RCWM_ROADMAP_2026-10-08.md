## 1. Top 8 (duplicates merged)

**1. Rule or trigger: a sealed generalisation map of the T2 ledger adapter** (eval Rank 1 + data 4). *Owner: RCWM; BWM mirrors it on RW2.*
- **Why:** training saw only posted/void statuses (375/126), amounts −9..12, |before| ≤ 98 and accounts A–D. Void going 0.04 → 1.00 cannot tell the rule apart from the token. This is the same pattern as BWM F-01.
- **Design:** no training. 13 strata × 100 one-step rows: unseen statuses (each with a posted twin), near-misses, inverted rule text, 4–5-digit balances, |amount| 13–500, 5/8 or renamed accounts, reordered JSON. Plus 80 k=4 chains. Arms: OFF, T2 s0–s2, Supa, copy floor. The new scorer must score 100% on the golds before any model output is read.
- **Payoff:** decides whether rule data must be surface-diversified before items 2–3 (part b: a status vocabulary plus held-out words). Also fixes PREREG-2's wording. **GPU** 1.5 (+1.5 for part b).
- **Confirm:** PREREG-3 frozen before any output. "Rule" if every unseen-status stratum is ≥ 0.80 (Wilson LB ≥ 0.70) in all seeds, with twins ≥ 0.80. "Trigger" if ≤ 0.30. Holm-adjusted McNemar.
- **Risk:** Titan's base semantics may already treat "pending" as a no-op. *Safeguard:* the OFF and Supa arms.

**2. Prerequisite: change the representation** (binary scratchpad + FlagWorld bridge + mechanism 2 P + P_bal). *Owner: both (RCWM runs it; BWM supplies the FlagWorld format).*
- **Why:**
  - Titan's P rule is integer addition (383/415 where that matches the bitwise answer, 2/333 where it does not).
  - T2 learned to copy: false copy 0.039 → 0.624; applicable state-changing 0.587 → 0.353; k4 exact 0.205 vs copy floor 0.117.
  - R15: deferral reaches only 0.10 on inapplicable steps.
- **Design:** ~1,500 fresh rows, a P-only LoRA on the T2 recipe, including 250 minimal-pair rows. An audit forces copy, apply-regardless, state+add and OR to each score 0.50 ± 0.02 on the training rows. The same rows are rendered three ways:
  - D-int: bare integer (the data control);
  - S-scratch: ~40-token bit scratchpad;
  - B-bits: bitstring plus an `applicable` flag.
  
  OOD test: 10- and 12-bit masks.
- **Payoff (uncertain):** applicable state-changing ≥ 0.85, false copy ≤ 0.25. **GPU** ~6.
- **Confirm:** a sealed P bank with dedupe that is invariant to bit permutation (the proposer reports 74% structural overlap in bank_confirm2; I have not verified this). Best format − D-int: k4 exact ≥ +0.20 and int-add-wrong stratum ≥ +0.50, Holm.
- **Risk:** a 0.6B model may not convert decimal to bits. *Safeguard:* B-bits avoids the conversion, and item 4 catches off-task bleed.

**3. Register: intermediates in the answer, with a filler control** (data 1 R + mechanism 2 R + micro-step). *Owner: RCWM; BWM mirrors it as D-27 A3 on RW2 legality.*
- **Why:** 175 mix rows taught nothing (0.022 → 0.022), while add/nowrap is 0.90. The conditional branch choice is 0.51–0.53, which is chance, even though the arithmetic is right. OFF outputs the unreduced sum (15/23). A per-step rate of 0.57 gives k4 exact of ~0.11.
- **Design:** 600 fresh rows (300 mix, 150 wrap, 150 conditional at thr−1/thr/thr+1). Each is rendered plain, as a trace, or with filler (same token count as the trace). R-only LoRA; the parser reads `after`.
- **Payoff:** mix ≥ 0.5, conditional ≥ 0.8, k4 regret 0.33 → ≤ 0.22. Speculative. **GPU** ~5.5.
- **Confirm:** go rule, in all 3 seeds: trace − plain ≥ +0.25 on conditional and ≥ +0.30 on mix, with filler below trace. Then a sealed R bank: k4 regret ≤ −0.10, reported at matched decode tokens.
- **Risk:** the gain may come from extra decode compute rather than supervision. *Safeguard:* the filler arm.

**4. A correctness-based retention suite with a fresh draw per use** (Rank 5). *Owner: both.*
- **Why:** the forgetting guard passed at 0.50×, yet cumulative drift is 1.94× and Supa/ON agreement is 0.37. KL never flagged Titan's abstention loss (p 6e-5). BWM's adapters have no retention evidence at all.
- **Design:** 8 code-graded categories × 75 items, chosen on a Supa-only dev seed. Every confirmation mints its own sealed draw. **GPU** 0.9, plus 0.2 per arm.
- **Confirm:** per-category McNemar, net loss ≤ 5/75, Holm. **Risk:** templated tasks are narrow. *Safeguard:* report the result as a floor.

**5. Dissect the T2 recipe** (mechanism 1 + mechanism 3 phase A + data 3). *Owner: RCWM; BWM shares the layer setting.*
- **Why:** only 24% of T2's update energy sits in blocks 8–15, where the skill is computed. 22% sits in blocks 0–7, which Titan's delta never touched. Void is already saturated. Posted cells that T2 never targeted rose 0.47 → 0.875, so oracle labels and frame adaptation have not been separated.
- **Design:** ledger only, one fresh dev bank:
  - LoRA confined to blocks 8–15, and separately to 16–27;
  - adapter scale α ∈ {0.25…1} (no training);
  - SD: trained on Titan's own answers;
  - NAT: trained on natural-marginal rows;
  - an r48 control only if the 8–15 arm underfits.
  
  **GPU** 3.5 screen + 2.5 confirmation.
- **Confirm:** on a sealed bank: 8–15 minus T2 regret ≤ +0.05; KL ratio upper bound < 0.80, plus item 4; T2 − SD regret ≤ −0.30.
- **Risk:** the 8–15 arm has fewer parameters, so a loss could be capacity, not locality. *Safeguard:* the r48 arm.

**6. Long horizons and seed-consensus certificates** (Rank 2 + Rank 3 + disagreement verification). *Owner: RCWM; BWM uses the result for horizon budgets.*
- **Why:** the product law holds at k ≤ 4 (0.684 predicted vs 0.683 observed). The three seeds agree on 85.1% of steps, and those steps are right 0.995 of the time. Majority voting (0.952) barely beats the best single seed, s2 (0.946). Horizon is confounded with magnitude: inputs above 99 are 12/25/44% of steps at k = 4/8/16.
- **Design:** no training. 120 sealed parents with 16-event plans, split into clamped and natural magnitude strata. s0 runs to k=16, OFF to k=8, and the voted ensemble vs s2 alone to k=8. Includes a noisy-oracle comparator. **GPU** ~3.
- **Confirm:** TOST equivalence within ±0.05 at k = 8/16; certificate precision lower bound ≥ 0.97 at ≥ 0.80 coverage. Registered prediction: consensus also certifies shortcuts the seeds share.
- **Risk:** 16-step plans may diverge so much that decisions become trivial. *Safeguard:* a pre-registered floor check.

**7. Sealed confirmation of BWM's deferral gate** (synergy part a). *Owner: BWM.*
- **Why:** F-03 (flip doubles 0.32 → 0.95; random deferral hurts) is single-seed and validation-grade only.
- **Design:** fresh RW2 keys at limits never used anywhere, with the band frozen at (0.01, 0.5). **GPU** ~1.
- **Confirm:** flips ≥ +0.30; in-distribution non-inferior at −0.01. **Risk:** the three-digit stratum is weaker. *Safeguard:* stratify by digit length.

**8. Is the LM's edge out of range? No-LLM learners at matched labels** (Rank 4 + data 5). *Owner: both; CPU only.*
- **Why:** S-roll reached ledger regret 0.051 against T2's 0.056 (measured on different banks), and it catches Titan by ~512 rows. PREREG-2 lists the missing comparator as a limit.
- **Design:** S-roll and a from-scratch digit transformer, trained on the exact 629 ledger rows Titan saw. Score them on the strata of items 1 and 6 that they can represent. Hash the learners before reading Titan's OOD outputs. **GPU** 0; keep the ≥ 12 GB RAM floor.
- **Confirm:** "edge" means Titan+T2 has ≥ 0.10 lower regret in ≥ 2 of 3 strata, with a symmetric "no-edge" rule. **Risk:** disputes over how well the baselines were tuned. *Safeguard:* a registered grid.

## 3. Do not do

1. **Dense-merge adapters, or sum them into an always-on core.** D-28(b) forbids it. The three T2 seeds behave almost identically yet have weight cosine of only 0.03, so weight orthogonality says nothing about interference.
2. **Tune on spent or reserved instruments:** pilot TEST, t2_eval/supp, R14's 248 (which include HSKILL-002-pool prompts), L1's 456 and the A-OOD bank. Never open HSKILL_CONFIRM_V2*.
3. **More ON/OFF deferral or seed voting for P or register.** R15 shows deferral only matches the base, and register any-of-3 coverage is just 0.62.
4. **Latent single-pass composition** (Rank 6, the jump adapter). Single-shot k4 exact is 0.000 on ledger and 0.005 on register.
5. **Self-label distillation now, or reporting tool tiers as model ability.** Oracle labels are free here, and calculators and parser-applied deltas are interface, not intelligence.
