# Research method and limitations

Research date: 5 October 2026. Scope: a targeted primary-source synthesis of consequence embeddings,
successor features, sequence/trajectory models, representation discovery, model-based control,
evaluation and compact-model integration. The source register has 43 entries, with review-depth labels.
Selected full-text methods were examined for Dreamer, successor features and DiSPO. Many surrounding
sources were inspected at the primary abstract, author-project or documentation level, not fully audited.

This is not an exhaustive search, a systematic meta-analysis or independent replication of the papers.
The closest prior art (including SF-GEN in language generation) narrows possible novelty. No numerical
benchmark advantage from a paper is treated as a forecast for Rineya. Earlier conversational statements
about newer papers' venues and percentage improvements are excluded unless verified here.

Historical Rineya context comes from the September 30 handbook and September 21 composable-skills
report available in the user's Library. No fresh repository audit, model loading or GPU experiment was
performed. The historical capture issue must be rechecked rather than assumed current.

Full third-party papers, datasets and software are not included. Links, original annotations, citation
metadata and implementation inspirations are supplied. Code/license/version review remains necessary
before adopting a referenced implementation. Newer preprints are future-reading evidence, not validated
Rineya dependencies.
