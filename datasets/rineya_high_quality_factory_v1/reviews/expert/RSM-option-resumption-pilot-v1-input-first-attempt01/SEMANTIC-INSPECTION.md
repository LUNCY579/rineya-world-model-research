## Concrete limits and shortcuts

The packet has 12 family join coordinates, 108 exact typed tasks, 216 distinct

program families or mechanism strata.

All 216 initial states are `(2,2)`. All twelve F executions share the first
seven complete events. All twelve R histories and snapshots are identical;
all twelve P full expected outputs are identical. That is a moderate diversity
and shortcut limitation. The family differences introduce genuine call/write/
branch/read structure, but most new program work is appended to a common
retained-argument/branch core. For expansion, vary actual initial/binding/cut
states and caller locations under matched controls; do not count the repeated
checkpoint or numeric/style variants as new mechanisms.

All **288 newly committed branch events are true**. All 456 newly entered
child invocations pass initiation and all 504 child returns pass success.
The 24 failed initiations are root I requests and 24 failed returns are root S
requests. This is a moderate coverage limitation: an always-true branch-output
rule fits observed selections, and child-failure propagation, executed else
branches and terminal-checkpoint resumption are not exercised by this actual
pilot. The false current guard at a suspended branch is nevertheless meaningful
and must not be mistaken for a newly selected false branch. Handmade tests
cover additional cases but do not increase corpus coverage. A future batch can
add matched opposite selections, nonroot failures and terminal checkpoints
without claiming those mechanisms are established here.

These are measured conditional cues, not trained-model performance. They do
not produce full event/checkpoint/next-event outputs. Only 75 full expected
