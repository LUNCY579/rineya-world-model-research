## Recommended next production order

1. **Broaden causal semantics and make new branches decisive.** Existing add, scale, transfer and swap already include multiplication and signed state, so it would be inaccurate to call the arithmetic entirely linear. They still form a narrow primitive set. A small new-contract pilot should add independently specified carry/wrap/modulo or other piecewise arithmetic, with exact boundary cases and simultaneous-update rules where appropriate. Cross each mechanism with applicable change, applicable identity, blocked precondition and rule-conditioned no-op cases. Before scaling, require matched examples where both branches of a newly added state condition alter execution. In the current expansion, removing the nested wrapper changes25 of60 development twin rule traces and21 final states, but the added state clause differs from its amount-only guard in only6 of60 tasks;32 guards are always true. Syntactic novelty is therefore larger than decisive state-composition coverage. Separate vocabulary, magnitude, serialization and predicate-composition factors. Evidence: `reviews/expert/semantic_inspection_expansion_v1.md`, `reviews/expert/verification_summary_expansion_v1.json`, and `reviews/adjudication/expansion-v1_scope_review.json`.

2. **Expand relational mechanisms, rather than only world size.** Preserve the reviewed partial-goal, resource-exhaustion, illegal-stop and canonical-tie cases, while adding new topology/dependency strata and rearranged initial states. Cross larger worlds with shorter/longer shortest horizons, alternative feasible routes and initially satisfied/unreachable controls; size alone is not difficulty. Larger independent block arrangements and explicitly defined logistics/resource dependencies are plausible new mechanisms, provided an independently authored solver verifies them. Keep successful submitted trajectories distinct from shortest trajectories: the expansion includes valid goal-reaching detours as well as optimal and empty solutions. Mechanism allocation should precede rendering, and source families must remain disjoint across splits and releases. Evidence: `reviews/expert/semantic_inspection_expansion_v1.md`, `reviews/expert/semantic_inspection_metadata_revisions.md`, and `reviews/adjudication/ADJUDICATION_EXPANSION_V3.md`.

3. **Extend critic faults beyond the current graph/fuel scaffold.** The new batch already supplies legal wrong-location claims; do not treat that gap as still absent. Next vary later missing-edge and dependency failures, legal wrong-state claims from different error producers, and objectively verified faults in new block/constraint/causal worlds. Each new world/objective type needs a public contract and independent oracle before generation. Balance fuel signs within plan-length/progress/goal strata, and report both matched support and full-pool frequencies. Preserve equal-length good/bad comparisons and cases where shorter invalid plans lose to longer valid ones. Candidate order/IDs should vary independently of quality. Full candidate-pool length associations remain measurable even with matched local controls; the small current development baseline is descriptive, not evidence of efficacy or complete shortcut removal. Evidence: `reviews/expert/semantic_inspection_expansion_v2.md`, `audits/shortcut_detection/claim-gap-v2-controls.json`, and `reviews/adjudication/ADJUDICATION_CLAIM_GAP_V2.md`.

4. **Deepen relevance, history and conflict in memory tasks.** Add queries for which a delayed update is actually decisive, including the missing as_of19 dependency, older authoritative versions, tombstones, equal-version disputes and irrelevant-event suppression. Exact-key matching currently defines relevance; a new pilot could supply explicit multi-attribute relevance rules, with answers requiring several records rather than incidental context length. Distinguish conflicts resolved by a declared policy from unresolved conflicts requiring a separately defined abstention/uncertainty answer. Put matched query outcomes across IDs, positions and temporal horizons, retaining the established rotation controls. These would extend visible finite retrieval data; they would not demonstrate persistent learned memory. Evidence: `schemas/EXTENDED_SEMANTICS.md` and `reviews/adjudication/ADJUDICATION_EXTENDED_V3.md`.

5. **Join actual skill execution with protection obligations.** Current RMM competence is declared, and its retention proxy protects supplied memory keys. Current RSM executes supplied procedures. A stronger small pilot should require dependent procedure composition, conditional selection, incomplete capability sets, abstention, competing routes and protected postconditions across several successful skill calls. Include AB versus BA cases where order changes the result, and cases where a superficially eligible route cannot satisfy the full declared obligation. Independently check every intermediate state and untouched protected value. This is an explicit execution/routing curriculum; it must not be described as measured acquisition or avoidance of forgetting. Evidence: `schemas/EXTENDED_SEMANTICS.md`, `research/curricula/RSM-001.md`, `research/curricula/RMM-001.md`, and the failed localization-acquisition finding F04 in `research/evidence_inventory/source_inventory.json`.

6. **Move belief tasks from uncertainty flags to information-gathering decisions.** The current can_reduce_uncertainty label identifies a possible distinguishing query but selects no information action and assigns no entropy objective. A new finite-world contract could define available observations, observation cost and a precise minimax or explicitly weighted objective. Supply cases where one query leaves ambiguity, another resolves it, and sequential query choice depends on the first answer. Include redundant, contradictory and unavailable observations, with exact strategy enumeration and canonical ties. Declared finite-world decision quality would be the target; Bayesian reasoning or learned uncertainty calibration would remain untested. Evidence: `schemas/EXTENDED_SEMANTICS.md` and `research/curricula/RBM-001.md`.

RJP, RDP and ROM should receive smaller mechanism pilots before count expansion: longer action sequences that expose predictive distinctions for RJP; genuinely dependent repair stages and changing constraints for RDP; and explicitly defined communication/observation or higher-order information custody for fictional ROM worlds. Preserve the existing observational-equivalence, minimum-Hamming/greedy-selection and explicit-policy boundaries. Do not relabel these finite tasks as latent, diffusion or psychological model success. Evidence: `schemas/EXTENDED_SEMANTICS.md` and `research/curricula/RJP-001.md`, `RDP-001.md`, `ROM-001.md`.
