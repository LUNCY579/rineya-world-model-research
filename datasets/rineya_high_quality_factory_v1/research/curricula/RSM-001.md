# RSM-001 — Hierarchical policies and reusable skills

Bundle priority P0; factory priority P0. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Execute procedures with initiation, arguments and termination. Compose subgoals in different orders and under conditions. Recover after interruption without replaying completed effects.

Semantic mechanisms: option_contract, argument_binding, conditional_subgoal, AB_BA_composition, interruption_resume, procedure_reuse, termination_check.

Oracle: Procedure interpreter plus primitive state simulator; verify initiation and termination, replay trace and final behavior; exact shortest path only where affordable.

Specialized rubric:

- Are option boundaries functional and explicit rather than arbitrary text chunks?
- Does more than one primitive mechanism affect the answer?
- Do argument bindings and stop conditions remain correct after interruption?
- Are unseen compositions held out at mechanism-template level?
- Are multiple legal implementations accepted under the same behavioral criteria?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: Single primitive procedure.
- Level 2: Same procedure with new arguments and renamed resources.
- Level 3: Conditional initiation or termination requiring multiple predicates.
- Level 4: Dependent subgoals executed in a sequence.
- Level 5: Novel AB/BA/conditional composition from separately taught procedures.
- Level 6: Interruption, redundant action and stale-subgoal traps.
- Level 7: Longer horizons and unseen composition DAGs in development challenges.

Measure complexity with: primitive_count, subgoal_count, composition_depth, horizon, interruptions.

Expand by: Add next-action, full-plan and recovery targets with shared lineage; count each underlying procedure family separately.

Scope limitation: No student-state aggregation is performed without authorized model execution; simulated off-path states are synthetic curriculum only.

Reuse: Extend the existing BWM search-distillation item; reuse RHD/CSS and layered-post-training infrastructure only after their branch interfaces and native status are checked.

Linked proposed experiments: E10, E11, E12. Resource anchors: P08, P09, P07, P34.
