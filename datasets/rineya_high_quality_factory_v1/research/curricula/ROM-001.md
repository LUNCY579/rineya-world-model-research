# ROM-001 — Social, opponent and character-belief models

Bundle priority P2; factory priority P2. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Maintain separate agent observations, beliefs and goals. Predict actions from agent-accessible information. Track explicit promises/relationship state while separating style from factual behavior.

Semantic mechanisms: false_belief, observation_access, belief_correction, promise_state, goal_conflict, cooperation.

Oracle: Finite fictional event/observation simulator and explicit decision policies; use set-valued targets for underdetermined human-like behavior.

Specialized rubric:

- Are world truth and each agent belief stored separately?
- Does the action require only information this agent observed or retrieved?
- Are inferred intentions uniquely determined by an explicit policy or allowed as a set?
- Are promises/corrections temporally consistent?
- Are style, instruction compliance and factual consistency evaluated separately?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: One agent knows or misses one event.
- Level 2: Identity renaming and equivalent observation wording.
- Level 3: Policy conditions depend on belief and promise/goal state.
- Level 4: Several events with delayed corrections and action consequences.
- Level 5: Cooperation among agents with different observations and goals.
- Level 6: Omniscient-information leakage and flattering but false style traps.
- Level 7: Held-out event combinations, partner policies and identities.

Measure complexity with: agent_count, event_count, belief_depth, goal_count, observation_asymmetry.

Expand by: Start original fictional agents and exact structured actions; add language realization after belief decisions pass independent review.

Scope limitation: General human social understanding and subjective style quality are not exact-oracle claims; ambiguous behavior must be quarantined or set-valued.

Reuse: Bridge to Rineya instruction-following and role-playing research. Use synthetic fictional profiles; no access to game manuscripts or private character files is needed for this research plan.

Linked proposed experiments: E28, E29, E30. Resource anchors: P20, P35, P21, P36.
