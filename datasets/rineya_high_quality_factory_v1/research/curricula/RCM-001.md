# RCM-001 — Causal and counterfactual rule models

Bundle priority P0; factory priority P0-A. Proposed initial target: 30 parents.

Learning objectives: Execute the supplied predicate rather than lexical priors. Separate applicability, exact computation and unchanged fields. Follow different meanings assigned to the same status/operator.

Semantic mechanisms: literal_status_mapping, rule_inversion, threshold_boundary, compound_precondition, same_answer_different_predicate, applicable_identity_vs_inapplicable_noop, multi_step_dependency.

Oracle: Independent AST predicate interpreter plus separately implemented arithmetic/state executor; integer arithmetic, complete state comparison and untouched-field invariants.

Specialized rubric:

- Does an explicit rule uniquely define exact string matching, case handling and precedence?
- Can the reviewer recompute applicability without seeing the generator expectation?
- Do counterfactual twins change only the declared intervention and retain a shared family key?
- Is applicable identity distinguished from inapplicable no-op and illegal action?
- Are equality, negative numbers and unfamiliar vocabulary included without answer hints?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: One literal applicability rule and one state update.
- Level 2: Same rule with novel names, numbers and input order; structural difficulty remains one step.
- Level 3: Two predicates or strict/equality threshold exceptions evaluated jointly.
- Level 4: Two to eight events where later applicability or arithmetic depends on an earlier result.
- Level 5: Compose a new rule selection, precondition and arithmetic procedure from separately taught primitives.
- Level 6: Matched token-trigger, false-copy and rule-reversal traps with no prompt/target leakage.
- Level 7: Development challenge holds out predicate trees, magnitude ranges and renderer combinations.

Measure complexity with: predicate_depth, condition_count, horizon, arithmetic_magnitude, rule_mapping_count.

Expand by: Balance apply/no-op at parent strata, cover exact threshold at -1/0/+1, and add composed predicates before increasing paraphrases.

Scope limitation: Hidden-causal discovery and arbitrary natural-language rule parsing require additional semantics and validation.

Reuse: Extend RCWM T2b and the joint cmp/calc/after kits; do not regenerate or retune their frozen banks. Existing generalization diagnostics are possible reuse candidates; remote availability needs confirmation.
