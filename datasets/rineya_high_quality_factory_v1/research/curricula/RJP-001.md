# RJP-001 — Latent predictive and energy-based models

Bundle priority P2; factory priority P2. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Identify decision-relevant invariant features without erasing exact bits. Predict masked structured features under declared transitions. Distinguish valid and near-miss candidate states.

Semantic mechanisms: lossless_abstraction, masked_feature_prediction, object_mask, invariance_pair, energy_candidate_labels, decision_critical_bit.

Oracle: Exact structured feature projection, decoder round-trip checks and transition verifier; define abstraction equivalence relative to declared goal/action set.

Specialized rubric:

- Is the abstraction sufficient for the stated decisions?
- Are exact equality, parity and arithmetic boundary bits preserved?
- Do positive pairs differ only in declared nuisance features?
- Are negative candidates invalid by independent execution?
- Are embeddings/energy/probability claims excluded from synthetic symbolic labels?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: Recover one masked feature from explicit rules.
- Level 2: Recognize equivalent states under nuisance renaming.
- Level 3: Condition feature prediction on multiple predicates.
- Level 4: Track projected features through dependent transitions.
- Level 5: Combine relational and arithmetic invariants without information loss.
- Level 6: Smooth-looking abstraction drops one decisive bit.
- Level 7: Held-out feature dependencies, object counts and rule combinations.

Measure complexity with: feature_count, mask_count, dependency_depth, horizon, equivalence_class_size.

Expand by: Produce structured contrastive and round-trip tasks; prioritize exact-bit negatives over large quantities of easy equivalences.

Scope limitation: Synthetic feature tasks are not trained JEPA/latent dynamics; latent efficiency and anti-collapse require later model experiments.

Reuse: Existing latent-head negatives in RCWM must be included. Tangent/JVP and recurrence infrastructure are potential later seams, not evidence of successful latent composition.

Linked proposed experiments: E22, E23, E24. Resource anchors: P16, P17, P29, P32.
