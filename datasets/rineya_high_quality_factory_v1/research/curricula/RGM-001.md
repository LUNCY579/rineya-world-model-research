# RGM-001 — Relational graphs and neuro-symbolic models

Bundle priority P0; factory priority P0-B. Proposed initial target: 30 parents.

Learning objectives: Represent typed relations independent of entity names or serialization. Check legal actions and exact state deltas. Construct and repair goal-reaching plans under explicit dynamics.

Semantic mechanisms: typed_graph_delta, precondition_legality, constraint_invariant, goal_path, invalid_plan_first_error, minimal_plan_repair, resource_dependency.

Oracle: Independent relation-set simulator plus graph invariants and bounded BFS/Dijkstra where optimality is claimed; replay every action and assert final goals.

Specialized rubric:

- Is the graph initially valid and are relation arities/types explicit?
- Does each action satisfy preconditions at its actual position in the trajectory?
- Are untouched relations preserved and mutual exclusions respected?
- Is feasibility checked separately from shortest-path optimality?
- Do entity renamings give isomorphic states and plans, including distractor objects?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: One edge/object relation update.
- Level 2: Entity-permuted isomorphic state and shuffled fact/key order.
- Level 3: Multiple relational preconditions or resource constraints.
- Level 4: A genuine multi-action path with dependent intermediate states.
- Level 5: Combine resource transfer, dependency release and relational movement.
- Level 6: A plausible plan with a subtle precondition violation plus a bounded verified repair.
- Level 7: Held-out graph motifs/object-count range crossed with a held-out renderer.

Measure complexity with: object_count, edge_count, dependency_depth, branching_factor, shortest_goal_distance, resource_count.

Expand by: Increase graph motifs and optimal goal distance after exact transition coverage; size alone does not ensure structural diversity.

Scope limitation: Large logistics/constraint search and minimum repairs need bounded exact solvers; record timeout as unknown, never unsolvable.

Reuse: Reuse BWM domains, planner interfaces and surface audit; preserve the completed 110-item hard result as historical evidence, not a tunable development set.
