## What RGM already covers and what remains narrow

All **216 distinct RGM tasks** exactly matched a new public-contract replay/search written in this execution. There are 126 graph tasks from 108 families and 90 block tasks from 72 families. Renderers produce 432 records, not 432 planning worlds. The audit was a retrospective public-semantics measurement; it does not replace original release reviews.

Graph coverage includes shortest horizons 0–7 or infeasible; 8 cases where fuel blocks a topological route, 20 where fuel lengthens the fresh shortest plan, 12 with multiple minimum-move routes, and 14 where the canonical minimum-move route consumes more fuel than the cheapest route. There are 36 submitted fuel failures, 12 missing-edge failures and 24 failures after a legal prefix. Seven legal goal-reaching submitted plans are longer than shortest. Directed/resource/multi-step cases are therefore present, not missing.

Block coverage includes minimum horizons 0–7, 37 partial goals, 18 blocked-source failures, 7 blocked-destination failures and 11 unchanged-support failures; 19 illegal plans follow a legal prefix. Sixty-six tasks have an initially illegal action become legal on the canonical optimal prefix, 76 admit an illegal adjacent swap of that optimal plan, and 10 require temporarily violating some initially satisfied goal relation. These are real dependency/reordering behaviors, not merely long traces.

The measured gaps are more specific:

- **No clean supplied input intervention pairs:** 36 source families contain two distinct tasks; 34 pairs change only the submitted plan and two change fuel **and** plan. There are zero fuel-only, edge-only or goal-only supplied family pairs. Existing state labels cannot demonstrate those matched input controls by themselves.
- **No valid block goal detours:** all 37 successful block submitted plans are minimum length. The data never shows a longer legal block plan achieving the same goal while the independently returned fresh minimum stays shorter. This can conflate plan success and optimality in this domain.
- **Concentrated block arrangements:** 90 tasks span only 14 initial support-shape isomorphism classes and 61 joint initial/partial-goal classes. Objects are limited to 3–5; there are no six-block states. A different entity name or goal on the same support shape does not create a new arrangement mechanism.
- **Size and split are coupled:** graph train tasks have 3–6 nodes, dev 7–8; block train tasks have 3–4 objects, dev 5. Exhaustive renaming invariants find 81 unweighted graph topologies and 14 block initial shapes with zero train/dev overlap, but the nonoverlap is partly forced by disjoint sizes. This is exposed size-range development coverage, not a sealed structural OOD or learning result.

Graph topology counts ignore costs, terminals and labels; weighted graph and initial-goal block counts use different invariants. These representation classes are not asserted to be independent mechanisms. Existing graph vocabulary variation, block partial goals, minimal fresh search and illegal-stop behavior must remain preserved. RGM provides a fresh minimum plan; RVM's repair is also fresh replacement. Neither is minimum edit-distance repair of a submitted plan. That latter target needs a separate typed contract and oracle, so it is not silently added here.

## Proposed next pilot: 48 genuinely fresh source families

Use the unchanged graph/block contract and NEW factual/control definitions in `schemas/RGM_PLANNING_CONTROL_PROPERTIES.md`. Allocate **12 A decisive-fuel families, 12 B directed-edge families, 12 C block-clearance/order families, and 12 D partial-goal obligation families**. The four explicit contexts in that public specification are supplied semantic controls, not four additional worlds counted independently. Cross two actual key/list-order renderers, producing a prospective **48 × 4 × 2 = 384 correlated rows**. This is a design count, not an achieved corpus.

Reserve unused source indices 180–227 only after a current active-lineage check confirms availability. The common `%10` rule gives 40 train and 8 exposed-dev families; four contiguous 12-family strata can each receive 10 train / 2 dev. All interventions, alternative plans, witnesses and surfaces remain in their family's split. If the actual policy differs, publish measured allocation before generation rather than overriding it silently. Successful complete review would bring RGM to **228 source families**; the other two P0 counts are unchanged.

Freshness gates apply before rendering: all 24 new graph base worlds must have distinct weighted directed topology with start/goal roles after exhaustive renaming, absent from approved prior base worlds; require at least 16 genuinely new unweighted topology classes across those 24. All 24 new block bases must have distinct joint initial/goal support incidence after renaming, absent from the prior corpus. Use twelve five-block and twelve six-block bases across C/D, including at least eight distinct six-block initial support shapes. A family key, cost perturbation, renderer, semantic control or goal-only change does not satisfy these gates by itself. Report collisions and actual achieved classes; if a requested class is infeasible, revise the prospective allocation publicly rather than weakening a frozen novelty claim.

Use graph sizes 5–7 across **both** splits and both graph strata; use five- and six-block states across both splits and both block strata. Each block stratum's two dev families should contain one of each size, with the ten train families evenly divided. Graph dev has only two families per stratum, so three sizes cannot all occur in each stratum's dev; balance the third size across A/B and publish that residual limitation. Mechanism and size should not be defined by split. No new primitive grammar is claimed: this wave grows fresh structural/state families and decisive controlled contrasts under two existing grammars.

## Honest difficulty and review bounds

Admit by actual search/dependency witnesses, not object count or submitted filler length. A two-move clear/non-goal context can remain Level 2; several constraints or a fuel boundary can be Level 3. Level 4 requires a genuinely dependent multi-action solution, including actual changed clearance or resource-sensitive fresh planning. The proposed C minimum horizons are 4–8; other horizons must be recorded from actual search. A legal detour is longer than optimal, not necessarily harder to find. There is no automatic Level 5/6 label, no new Level 7 sealed challenge and no acquisition/efficacy claim.

Bound graph sizes to seven nodes and fuel to 30, submitted trajectories to 12 moves, and blocks to six objects. Independently enumerate finite state spaces for optimality, retaining completion/frontier counts. Reject contradictory block goals as malformed input. Search timeout is `UNKNOWN`, not unreachable. If resource/clearance controls cannot be witnessed within these bounds, redesign the family before freezing it; do not compensate with extra aliases.

The proposed four-context layout stays small enough to inspect all 48 families. Require exact targets and read-back under both renderers, all supplied pair field-difference checks, fully executed audit probes, and completed separate expert/red reviews with input-derived predictions frozen first. Conditional histograms must report legality, goal achievement, fresh-plan feasibility/length, first-error index/reason, object/edge count, plan length and fuel boundary by family, stratum, split and renderer. Measure input-only size/length/fuel/edge and token baselines; rowwise metadata tags and random shuffling are not substitute evidence. Mixed support where feasible must be measured; any remaining deterministic cohort cue or conditional association is reported explicitly. Require all four supplied contexts per accepted family; passing one member does not discharge a failed required pair.

## Subsequent gaps kept separate

Resource consumption here is one scalar fuel budget; there is no transfer, replenishment, coupled inventory, task-release prerequisite or logistics capacity. Such mechanisms could motivate a later 32-family new-contract pilot, but need explicit typed state/action/goal semantics, precedence and failure rules, independent finite solvers and public regression properties before production. Block/graph edit-distance repair likewise needs an explicit edit alphabet, cost, tie rule and bound. This proposal does not invent those targets to inflate mechanism counts.

The evidence supports bounded proxy data coverage only. It establishes neither neuro-symbolic integration, learned planning, reusable procedure acquisition, trained critic quality nor model improvement. Existing research motivation and historical BWM evidence remain unchanged. This author helped design earlier public controls and adjudicated earlier RGM releases, and may later adjudicate this pilot only after completed first reviews; that overlap and shared underlying-model error risk must remain disclosed.
