# RVM-001 — Validity critics, value models and successor features

Bundle priority P0; factory priority P0-C. Proposed initial target: 30 parents.

Learning objectives: Label transition validity, goal progress and utility separately. Locate the first objectively invalid step. Rank candidate plans using explicit goal and cost assumptions.

Semantic mechanisms: transition_validity, first_error_location, goal_progress, minimal_repair, cost_preference, goal_weight_change, near_miss_arithmetic.

Oracle: Independently recompute candidate trajectories and exact objective values; derive a partial order or tie when candidates cannot be uniquely ranked.

Specialized rubric:

- Does every validity label follow exact dynamics?
- Are progress and usefulness defined by visible goal/cost, rather than verbosity?
- Are chosen/rejected directions correct and ties retained explicitly?
- Can error-producer identity, answer length or explanation style predict preference?
- Is the first invalid step separated from downstream consequences?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: One correct/incorrect state update.
- Level 2: Matched correct and incorrect candidates with equal format/length.
- Level 3: Validity and progress disagree under a conditional goal.
- Level 4: Identify first error in a two-to-eight-step trajectory.
- Level 5: Rank plans across multiple verified constraints and explicit costs.
- Level 6: A fluent/short/verbose near miss with matched correctness controls.
- Level 7: Held-out corruption producers, goal functions and mechanism combinations.

Measure complexity with: trajectory_length, candidate_count, error_position, constraint_count, goal_terms.

Expand by: Add ties, valid-but-unhelpful and invalid-but-apparently-progressing cases before expanding preference exports.

Scope limitation: A generic value model or successor-feature guarantee cannot be inferred from local objective labels; fixed-dynamics assumptions must be stated.

Reuse: Reuse RCWM goal-factorized comparisons and queued verification-guided rollout. The joint roadmap reports a blanket-verifier regression; that is a mandatory baseline, not a reason to assume a new verifier helps.
