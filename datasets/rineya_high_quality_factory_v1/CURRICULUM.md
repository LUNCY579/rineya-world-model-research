# Dataset curriculum v1

Status: proposed dataset specification. This architect generated no examples and approved no samples. Counts are targets; actual coverage belongs in the production reports. No training or new efficacy experiment was run.

The first sequence is RCM-001, RGM-001 and RVM-001. RSM is next on the bundle P0 path; memory, routing and beliefs can begin small exact pilots after shared review works. Latent, revision and social tracks remain bounded by verification.

## Difficulty and coverage discipline

- **Level 1 — Basic competence:** One unambiguous semantic mechanism.
- **Level 2 — Controlled generalization:** Surface/numerical variants without increased dependency structure.
- **Level 3 — Conditional reasoning:** Multiple conditions, preconditions or exceptions.
- **Level 4 — Multi-step reasoning:** Later operations depend on earlier results; independent repetitions do not qualify.
- **Level 5 — Compositional transfer:** At least two independently defined mechanisms interact; novel compositions are held out by template.
- **Level 6 — Adversarial robustness:** Explicit shortcut/near-miss challenge axis, recorded alongside base structural level.
- **Level 7 — OOD development challenge:** Partition-relative unseen range, motif, rule or combined shift; recorded alongside base structural level.

Store structural complexity (conditions, dependencies, graph size, horizon and composition depth) separately from adversarial and partition-relative OOD tags. A one-step renamed task remains Level 2; a conjunction may be Level 3; calling an ordinary task “hard” does not make it compositional. Levels 6 and 7 also record the underlying Level 1-5 structure. Longer prompts alone are never a difficulty measure.

Start with approximately 30 independent parents per P0-A/B/C track, at least three parents per supported mechanism and at most four initial renderings per parent. For a 150-250-parent expansion, a proposed Level 1-5 mix is 10/15/25/30/20%, but unsupported levels must remain zero and documented gaps. Counterfactual twins, trajectory variants and paraphrases share family custody; their renderings do not inflate independent concept counts.

Target both sides of applicability and changes at parent strata. Where possible include at least 25% declared counterfactual, near-miss or shortcut controls. A proposed quota is not a claim of achieved balance. Challenge partitions must hold out actual mechanisms/templates or combined shifts, not merely random seeds. No dataset producer creates or opens a final sealed test.

## Common review gate

- Input completeness and clarity.
- Unambiguous requested behavior or explicit valid-answer set.
- Independent executable target agreement.
- Semantic coherence and initial-state validity.
- Intended competency genuinely required.
- No gold or outcome encoded in student metadata.
- Family-level lineage and partition integrity.
- Rights/provenance documented.
- No unresolved correctness or adversarial defect.

The formal oracle, blind expert, adversarial reviewer and adjudicator must execute separately. A correct deterministic generator cannot approve itself. Multiple valid answers require an explicit equivalence/set-based validator; unresolved uncertainty is quarantined.

## RCM-001 — Causal and counterfactual rule models

Bundle priority P0; factory priority P0-A. Proposed initial target: 30 parents.

Learning objectives: Execute the supplied predicate rather than lexical priors. Separate applicability, exact computation and unchanged fields. Follow different meanings assigned to the same status/operator.

Semantic mechanisms: literal_status_mapping, rule_inversion, threshold_boundary, compound_precondition, same_answer_different_predicate, applicable_identity_vs_inapplicable_noop, multi_step_dependency.

Oracle: Independent AST predicate interpreter plus separately implemented arithmetic/state executor; integer arithmetic, complete state comparison and untouched-field invariants.

Specialized rubric:

- Does an explicit rule uniquely define exact string matching, case handling and precedence?
- Can the reviewer recompute applicability without seeing the generator expectation?
- Do counterfactual twins change only the declared intervention and retain a shared family key?
- Is applicable identity distinguished from inapplicable no-op and illegal action?
- Are equality, negative numbers and unfamiliar vocabulary included without answer hints?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: One literal applicability rule and one state update.
- Level 2: Same rule with novel names, numbers and input order; structural difficulty remains one step.
- Level 3: Two predicates or strict/equality threshold exceptions evaluated jointly.
- Level 4: Two to eight events where later applicability or arithmetic depends on an earlier result.
- Level 5: Compose a new rule selection, precondition and arithmetic procedure from separately taught primitives.
- Level 6: Matched token-trigger, false-copy and rule-reversal traps with no prompt/target leakage.
- Level 7: Development challenge holds out predicate trees, magnitude ranges and renderer combinations.

Measure complexity with: predicate_depth, condition_count, horizon, arithmetic_magnitude, rule_mapping_count.

Expand by: Balance apply/no-op at parent strata, cover exact threshold at -1/0/+1, and add composed predicates before increasing paraphrases.

Scope limitation: Hidden-causal discovery and arbitrary natural-language rule parsing require additional semantics and validation.

Reuse: Extend RCWM T2b and the joint cmp/calc/after kits; do not regenerate or retune their frozen banks. Existing generalization diagnostics are possible reuse candidates; remote availability needs confirmation.

## RGM-001 — Relational graphs and neuro-symbolic models

Bundle priority P0; factory priority P0-B. Proposed initial target: 30 parents.

Learning objectives: Represent typed relations independent of entity names or serialization. Check legal actions and exact state deltas. Construct and repair goal-reaching plans under explicit dynamics.

Semantic mechanisms: typed_graph_delta, precondition_legality, constraint_invariant, goal_path, invalid_plan_first_error, minimal_plan_repair, resource_dependency.

Oracle: Independent relation-set simulator plus graph invariants and bounded BFS/Dijkstra where optimality is claimed; replay every action and assert final goals.

Specialized rubric:

- Is the graph initially valid and are relation arities/types explicit?
- Does each action satisfy preconditions at its actual position in the trajectory?
- Are untouched relations preserved and mutual exclusions respected?
- Is feasibility checked separately from shortest-path optimality?
- Do entity renamings give isomorphic states and plans, including distractor objects?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: One edge/object relation update.
- Level 2: Entity-permuted isomorphic state and shuffled fact/key order.
- Level 3: Multiple relational preconditions or resource constraints.
- Level 4: A genuine multi-action path with dependent intermediate states.
- Level 5: Combine resource transfer, dependency release and relational movement.
- Level 6: A plausible plan with a subtle precondition violation plus a bounded verified repair.
- Level 7: Held-out graph motifs/object-count range crossed with a held-out renderer.

Measure complexity with: object_count, edge_count, dependency_depth, branching_factor, shortest_goal_distance, resource_count.

Expand by: Increase graph motifs and optimal goal distance after exact transition coverage; size alone does not ensure structural diversity.

Scope limitation: Large logistics/constraint search and minimum repairs need bounded exact solvers; record timeout as unknown, never unsolvable.

Reuse: Reuse BWM domains, planner interfaces and surface audit; preserve the completed 110-item hard result as historical evidence, not a tunable development set.

## RVM-001 — Validity critics, value models and successor features

Bundle priority P0; factory priority P0-C. Proposed initial target: 30 parents.

Learning objectives: Label transition validity, goal progress and utility separately. Locate the first objectively invalid step. Rank candidate plans using explicit goal and cost assumptions.

Semantic mechanisms: transition_validity, first_error_location, goal_progress, minimal_repair, cost_preference, goal_weight_change, near_miss_arithmetic.

Oracle: Independently recompute candidate trajectories and exact objective values; derive a partial order or tie when candidates cannot be uniquely ranked.

Specialized rubric:

- Does every validity label follow exact dynamics?
- Are progress and usefulness defined by visible goal/cost, rather than verbosity?
- Are chosen/rejected directions correct and ties retained explicitly?
- Can error-producer identity, answer length or explanation style predict preference?
- Is the first invalid step separated from downstream consequences?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: One correct/incorrect state update.
- Level 2: Matched correct and incorrect candidates with equal format/length.
- Level 3: Validity and progress disagree under a conditional goal.
- Level 4: Identify first error in a two-to-eight-step trajectory.
- Level 5: Rank plans across multiple verified constraints and explicit costs.
- Level 6: A fluent/short/verbose near miss with matched correctness controls.
- Level 7: Held-out corruption producers, goal functions and mechanism combinations.

Measure complexity with: trajectory_length, candidate_count, error_position, constraint_count, goal_terms.

Expand by: Add ties, valid-but-unhelpful and invalid-but-apparently-progressing cases before expanding preference exports.

Scope limitation: A generic value model or successor-feature guarantee cannot be inferred from local objective labels; fixed-dynamics assumptions must be stated.

Reuse: Reuse RCWM goal-factorized comparisons and queued verification-guided rollout. The joint roadmap reports a blanket-verifier regression; that is a mandatory baseline, not a reason to assume a new verifier helps.

Linked proposed experiments: E04, E05, E06. Resource anchors: P03, P04, P22, P26.

## RSM-001 — Hierarchical policies and reusable skills

Bundle priority P0; factory priority P0. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Execute procedures with initiation, arguments and termination. Compose subgoals in different orders and under conditions. Recover after interruption without replaying completed effects.

Semantic mechanisms: option_contract, argument_binding, conditional_subgoal, AB_BA_composition, interruption_resume, procedure_reuse, termination_check.

Oracle: Procedure interpreter plus primitive state simulator; verify initiation and termination, replay trace and final behavior; exact shortest path only where affordable.

Specialized rubric:

- Are option boundaries functional and explicit rather than arbitrary text chunks?
- Does more than one primitive mechanism affect the answer?
- Do argument bindings and stop conditions remain correct after interruption?
- Are unseen compositions held out at mechanism-template level?
- Are multiple legal implementations accepted under the same behavioral criteria?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: Single primitive procedure.
- Level 2: Same procedure with new arguments and renamed resources.
- Level 3: Conditional initiation or termination requiring multiple predicates.
- Level 4: Dependent subgoals executed in a sequence.
- Level 5: Novel AB/BA/conditional composition from separately taught procedures.
- Level 6: Interruption, redundant action and stale-subgoal traps.
- Level 7: Longer horizons and unseen composition DAGs in development challenges.

Measure complexity with: primitive_count, subgoal_count, composition_depth, horizon, interruptions.

Expand by: Add next-action, full-plan and recovery targets with shared lineage; count each underlying procedure family separately.

Scope limitation: No student-state aggregation is performed without authorized model execution; simulated off-path states are synthetic curriculum only.

Reuse: Extend the existing BWM search-distillation item; reuse RHD/CSS and layered-post-training infrastructure only after their branch interfaces and native status are checked.

Linked proposed experiments: E10, E11, E12. Resource anchors: P08, P09, P07, P34.

## REM-001 — Episodic, associative and procedural memory

Bundle priority P1; factory priority P1. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Retrieve decisive evidence with event time and provenance. Apply correction/supersession without stale-fact errors. Ignore irrelevant memory and abstain on unresolved conflict.

Semantic mechanisms: relevant_retrieval, timestamp_update, supersession, source_authority, unresolved_conflict, delayed_dependency, procedure_memory.

Oracle: Timestamped event ledger with explicit authority/supersession policy; independent query resolver outputs evidence IDs and answer/unknown.

Specialized rubric:

- Does the correct answer require retrieval rather than being repeated in the query?
- Are event time and statement time distinguished?
- Is source authority or supersession policy explicit for conflicts?
- Are supporting facts available before the query, with no future evidence?
- Can the reader identify evidence and uncertainty without memorizing names?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: Retrieve one visible historical fact.
- Level 2: Rename entities and paraphrase queries with equal evidence.
- Level 3: Resolve one explicit update or authority-conditioned conflict.
- Level 4: Combine separated records and delayed dependencies.
- Level 5: Retrieve a stored procedure and execute it using current corrected facts.
- Level 6: Stale summaries, recent distractors and unsupported confidence traps.
- Level 7: Held-out update patterns, longer histories and changed procedure combinations.

Measure complexity with: history_length, relevant_record_count, distractor_count, update_depth, time_gap.

Expand by: Increase genuine corrections and unresolved conflicts; vary chronology independently of answer labels and keep provenance in evidence targets.

Scope limitation: Storage compression, learned write policies and dense fact consolidation are model/system hypotheses, not demonstrated memory gains.

Reuse: Prior history identifies RHF-001 on research/rineya-hebbian-fact-memory-v1 and factorized/triadic memory branches. These overlaps were not fully re-audited here; verify and reuse before coding. Do not label prior synthetic memory results as native gains.

Linked proposed experiments: E19, E20, E21. Resource anchors: P13, P14, P15, P27.

Sources: CARD_REM-001, TRACKS, REGISTRY, PREDICTIONS, RESOURCES, PROTOCOL, CONTRACTS, REUSE. Benefits remain hypotheses.

## RMM-001 — Modular experts, continual learning and meta-learning

Bundle priority P1; factory priority P1. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Select an operation-appropriate competent skill from explicit qualifications. Abstain when no supplied skill supports the rule. Track old and new skill requirements without role mixing.

Semantic mechanisms: operation_specific_selection, competence_mask, abstention, skill_sequence_replay, few_shot_rule_induction, role_separation.

Oracle: Finite competence registry and explicit routing/cost constraints; rule induction accepted only when support uniquely identifies query behavior.

Specialized rubric:

- Are competence statements visible evidence rather than claimed empirical model performance?
- Does routing match the requested operation, not just domain tokens?
- Can no expert solve the task, and is abstention the correct answer then?
- Is claimed few-shot rule induction identifiable from support examples?
- Are retention records synthetic task obligations rather than measured model retention?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: Select one visibly qualified skill for one operation.
- Level 2: Rename skill IDs and reorder registry entries.
- Level 3: Combine competence, cost and applicability constraints.
- Level 4: Route a sequence while preserving prior obligations.
- Level 5: Compose predictor/proposer/critic procedures with distinct roles.
- Level 6: Conflicting qualifications, attractive incompetent expert and unsupported-rule traps.
- Level 7: Held-out qualification patterns, task mixtures and rule families.

Measure complexity with: expert_count, operation_count, constraint_count, sequence_length, support_examples.

Expand by: Provide synthetic routing and support/query episodes first; defer learning-curve or retention claims to separate model studies.

Scope limitation: True forgetting, learned router drift and adaptation efficacy cannot be measured by dataset construction.

Reuse: IDR-001 README was verified remotely: implemented and CPU-tested, native Rineya/CUDA efficacy unqualified. Reuse research/rineya-id-balancing-skill-routing-v1 rather than creating a duplicate router.

Linked proposed experiments: E13, E14, E15. Resource anchors: P10, P11, P25, P31.

## RBM-001 — Belief-state models and metacognitive control

Bundle priority P1; factory priority P1. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Update a belief set from permitted observations. Distinguish known, unknown and conflicting information. Choose inspection/abstention when it has explicit expected utility.

Semantic mechanisms: partial_observation, set_belief_update, bayesian_update, information_action, contradictory_evidence, observation_delay.

Oracle: Enumerate finite hidden states using exact rational probabilities where stated; independent observation-consistency filter and explicit observation-cost model.

Specialized rubric:

- Is latent truth excluded from the student-visible lane?
- Are likelihood/prior assumptions stated when a numeric posterior is required?
- Does ambiguity remain when observations are insufficient?
- Are inspection value and cost defined, including tie/abstain behavior?
- Are contradictory signals governed by a noise/authority rule?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: Identify one unknown field from an incomplete observation.
- Level 2: Equivalent observation encodings and renamed hidden states.
- Level 3: Update multiple constraints or one explicit noisy observation.
- Level 4: Accumulate evidence over several observation/action steps.
- Level 5: Combine memory retrieval, belief update and information gathering.
- Level 6: Confident shared-shortcut and misleading-observation traps.
- Level 7: Held-out noise/delay regimes and hidden-state structures.

Measure complexity with: hidden_state_count, observation_count, belief_support_size, history_length, inspection_cost.

Expand by: Begin finite deterministic belief sets; add rational Bayes only after enumeration parity, then utility of inspect versus answer.

Scope limitation: Calibration and learned introspective confidence require model predictions; oracle beliefs are a privileged ceiling.

Reuse: Reuse existing abstention/uncertainty work cautiously. The failed prerequisite deferral is a required negative case. Connect later to adaptive-depth work only after finite-compute benefit is measured.

Linked proposed experiments: E16, E17, E18. Resource anchors: P12, P26, P28.

## RJP-001 — Latent predictive and energy-based models

Bundle priority P2; factory priority P2. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Identify decision-relevant invariant features without erasing exact bits. Predict masked structured features under declared transitions. Distinguish valid and near-miss candidate states.

Semantic mechanisms: lossless_abstraction, masked_feature_prediction, object_mask, invariance_pair, energy_candidate_labels, decision_critical_bit.

Oracle: Exact structured feature projection, decoder round-trip checks and transition verifier; define abstraction equivalence relative to declared goal/action set.

Specialized rubric:

- Is the abstraction sufficient for the stated decisions?
- Are exact equality, parity and arithmetic boundary bits preserved?
- Do positive pairs differ only in declared nuisance features?
- Are negative candidates invalid by independent execution?
- Are embeddings/energy/probability claims excluded from synthetic symbolic labels?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: Recover one masked feature from explicit rules.
- Level 2: Recognize equivalent states under nuisance renaming.
- Level 3: Condition feature prediction on multiple predicates.
- Level 4: Track projected features through dependent transitions.
- Level 5: Combine relational and arithmetic invariants without information loss.
- Level 6: Smooth-looking abstraction drops one decisive bit.
- Level 7: Held-out feature dependencies, object counts and rule combinations.

Measure complexity with: feature_count, mask_count, dependency_depth, horizon, equivalence_class_size.

Expand by: Produce structured contrastive and round-trip tasks; prioritize exact-bit negatives over large quantities of easy equivalences.

Scope limitation: Synthetic feature tasks are not trained JEPA/latent dynamics; latent efficiency and anti-collapse require later model experiments.

Reuse: Existing latent-head negatives in RCWM must be included. Tangent/JVP and recurrence infrastructure are potential later seams, not evidence of successful latent composition.

## RDP-001 — Diffusion and revisable proposal models

Bundle priority P2; factory priority P2. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Identify and repair bounded candidate errors. Preserve valid candidate prefixes and avoid introducing new violations. Distinguish semantic solution diversity from surface variation.

Semantic mechanisms: first_fault_repair, bounded_edit, prefix_preservation, iterative_revision, multi_candidate_selection, repair_tie.

Oracle: Replay candidate against independent simulator; bounded edit enumeration/BFS defines repair feasibility and minimality only within declared cost/bound.

Specialized rubric:

- Does the original candidate actually contain the alleged defect?
- Is each corrected plan executable and goal-reaching?
- Is minimality proved within a visible edit metric/bound?
- Are alternative equally good repairs accepted?
- Does an extra revision create a regression or only reword the same plan?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: Repair one corrupted arithmetic/transition step.
- Level 2: Repair renamed equivalent candidates.
- Level 3: Repair a failed precondition with explicit constraints.
- Level 4: Repair the earliest failure and dependent suffix of a plan.
- Level 5: Repair plans composed of several reusable procedures.
- Level 6: Tempting local fix that breaks later constraints.
- Level 7: Held-out fault combinations, horizons and graph motifs.

Measure complexity with: edit_distance, fault_count, suffix_dependency_depth, candidate_count, plan_length.

Expand by: Add shared-start-candidate repair comparisons, ties and unrepairable-within-bound labels before masked revision tasks.

Scope limitation: A verified revision dataset is not a diffusion model; denoising speed, diversity and learning benefit remain unmeasured.

Reuse: Coordinate with existing repeated-sampling/inference-compute experiments before making another proposer harness. No new model downloads or paid APIs are required by this bundle.

Linked proposed experiments: E25, E26, E27. Resource anchors: P18, P19, P32.

## ROM-001 — Social, opponent and character-belief models

Bundle priority P2; factory priority P2. Proposed initial target: 12 parents.

Learning objectives: Maintain separate agent observations, beliefs and goals. Predict actions from agent-accessible information. Track explicit promises/relationship state while separating style from factual behavior.

Semantic mechanisms: false_belief, observation_access, belief_correction, promise_state, goal_conflict, cooperation.

Oracle: Finite fictional event/observation simulator and explicit decision policies; use set-valued targets for underdetermined human-like behavior.

Specialized rubric:

- Are world truth and each agent belief stored separately?
- Does the action require only information this agent observed or retrieved?
- Are inferred intentions uniquely determined by an explicit policy or allowed as a set?
- Are promises/corrections temporally consistent?
- Are style, instruction compliance and factual consistency evaluated separately?

Curriculum:

- Level 1: One agent knows or misses one event.
- Level 2: Identity renaming and equivalent observation wording.
- Level 3: Policy conditions depend on belief and promise/goal state.
- Level 4: Several events with delayed corrections and action consequences.
- Level 5: Cooperation among agents with different observations and goals.
- Level 6: Omniscient-information leakage and flattering but false style traps.
- Level 7: Held-out event combinations, partner policies and identities.

Measure complexity with: agent_count, event_count, belief_depth, goal_count, observation_asymmetry.

Expand by: Start original fictional agents and exact structured actions; add language realization after belief decisions pass independent review.

Scope limitation: General human social understanding and subjective style quality are not exact-oracle claims; ambiguous behavior must be quarantined or set-valued.

Reuse: Bridge to Rineya instruction-following and role-playing research. Use synthetic fictional profiles; no access to game manuscripts or private character files is needed for this research plan.

Linked proposed experiments: E28, E29, E30. Resource anchors: P20, P35, P21, P36.
