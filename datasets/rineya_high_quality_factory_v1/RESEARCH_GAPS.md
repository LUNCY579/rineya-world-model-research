## Expansion decisions

Expand the three initial tracks toward 150-250 accepted independent parents only after the entire pipeline passes actual review. Add missing semantic mechanisms before more surface variants. Prefer multi-step dependency and compositional templates after one-step conditional labels are reliable. Maintain worst-stratum, label and difficulty coverage alongside total volume.

RSM should add true procedure composition and recovery. REM/RBM require independent time/conflict/observation semantics. RMM may produce exact routing episodes, but dataset creation cannot establish retention. RJP/RDP/ROM should begin with lossless abstractions, bounded repair and explicit fictional policies; broader architecture/social claims remain unmeasured.

The 35 registry experiments and 13 bundle predictions are planned, unmeasured source records. GPU caps and model advancement thresholds in them are future study designs; this CPU dataset role executes none of those studies.
