# Bounded generator-v2 design and DEV pre-registration

11 October 2026, America/Chicago. This is a future architecture hypothesis, not an implemented change or promotion approval. Only this report was written. No generator, validator, configuration, database, accepted shard, or sealed gold was changed; no sealed labels were opened. No model training, inference service, or paid API was used. The current resource pause remains in force: approximately 9 GiB free is below the configured 10 GiB publishing floor. Even the small pilot described here must not publish while that gate fails.

The source baseline is the 31-file Python/configuration snapshot with digest `3249586daa51d27188636dfe7e342ad8661ea51fbf40afdc7ef3a2becb738b12`. The measured [surface comparison](CONTROLLED_SURFACE_COMPARISON.md) checked 616 actual DEV/OOD v1 records and 1,848 rendered listwise variants. The rendered surfaces preserve exact labels and reduce one position correlation, but the C candidate-relationship heuristic remains best-membership correct for 11/11 DEV parents and 10/11 OOD parents. It chooses a plan with a supplied one-step-shorter prefix and exact reverse, resolving multiple qualifiers by opaque ID; it reads no goal, initial state, rules, or labels. V1 entity/status aliases also repeat across splits. These two observed gaps define the next experiment.

## Scope and hypotheses

The next bounded phase has two separate arms. Arm C tests whether changing only the goal over a fixed candidate bundle removes the measured goal-blind construction correlation. Arm V tests consistent, split-reserved entity/status renaming, with familiar-vocabulary controls kept explicitly separate from held-out-vocabulary observations. Neither arm tests learned model generalization, unbounded planning, causal transfer, or the full A-H production curriculum.

Arm C hypothesis: three equally weighted, distinct non-initial terminal goals over the same deterministic world and candidate plans produce disjoint exact best sets. Any deterministic selector using only the candidate bundle makes the same selection for all three goals and can have at most one-third best-membership correctness. The measured prefix/reverse heuristic is one such selector. This claim concerns one selected candidate, not a predictor allowed to return all candidates or a goal-aware model.

Arm V hypothesis: a declared bijection of entity/status namespaces changes exact surface strings while preserving normalized dynamics and exact utility/ranking/probability labels. Every held-out DEV surface string is absent from the TRAIN reserved lexicon. This is a holdout of complete strings; it does not establish unseen tokenizer pieces or unfamiliar natural-language meanings.

## C bundle: multiple reachable goals without an initial-state cue

Use the existing DEV structural reservation: four blocks, valid acyclic support forests, horizon four, the existing clear-block move rules, exact full-state goal atoms, and the unchanged lexicographic utility. Keep the initial state, rules, constraints, instruction, candidate IDs, candidate plans, and candidate order identical across all goals in a bundle. Derive the parent identity from the versioned underlying world/rules/candidate bundle before choosing a goal or vocabulary presentation.

For one parent, propose up to 24 legal four-move paths from the same initial forest using a seeded, declared proposal order. Keep the first three whose terminal forests are pairwise distinct and all differ from the initial forest; do not choose endpoints using favorable decision labels. Call the paths P0, P1, and P2. All endpoint paths have the same length and move-cost schedule. The supplied candidate set contains those three paths, P0's one-step-shorter prefix, P0's exact reverse, and the empty plan: six candidates, each within the existing plan-length budget. Reverse execution may be illegal; it must be simulated, never assumed valid.

Emit three full-forest goals, respectively equal to the terminal forests of P0, P1, and P2. Each has four `on` atoms. None equals the initial state, so the empty plan cannot complete any goal. Preserve the prefix/reverse pattern deliberately: the controlled contrast tests whether the goal changes its decision relevance, rather than claiming that a superficially different candidate set defeats the shortcut.

Evaluate every candidate under every goal with both executable oracles. A legal goal-reaching candidate exists by construction. Because deterministic terminal forests are distinct and goals specify the complete forest, an individual feasible candidate can satisfy all four goal atoms for at most one of the three goals. With feasibility and utility semantics unchanged, the three exact best sets should be pairwise disjoint. Cheaper prefixes or tied paths reaching the same goal are allowed and must remain in the complete best set; do not filter them to manufacture a desired candidate-role winner.

All three tasks receive new canonical record IDs. Candidate IDs remain the same within the bundle, but must be freshly assigned independently of goal labels. Candidate permutation is computed once per parent, without gold or goal index, and reused across the three tasks. Ordinary per-record winner rotation must not be applied here: it changes the candidate ordering along with the goal and compromises a clean goal-only contrast.

The primary diagnostic uses canonical candidate bundles and the established order-invariant prefix/reverse heuristic. If rendered rows are subsequently published, their goal-only control requires a v2 presentation policy keyed by the parent/bundle permutation seed rather than the goal-specific `record_index`. The existing renderer's seed-plus-record-index ordering should retain its exact v1 behavior and version identity. A new rendering policy needs a new rendering version and new derivative IDs; existing rendered bytes remain immutable. Key/whitespace presentation variants are repeated observations of one parent, not additional independent samples.

## Split-reserved vocabulary

Declare finite TRAIN, DEV, and OOD lexicons before sampling evaluation outcomes. The sets must be disjoint for each namespace: accounts, entries, blocks, registers, ledger status surfaces, and the special table-support surface where it is renamed. Draw opaque strings from the same alphabet and length distribution across splits; avoid `train_`, `dev_`, semantic-role names, or other visible split labels. Use declared parent-seeded permutations so a surface does not permanently encode a semantic status role.

Rename namespace references explicitly and consistently. Accounts/entries must change in initial and possible-world states, goals, constraints, and action arguments. Blocks must change in support forests, destinations, goals, and constraints; the table sentinel must remain distinct from every block. Registers must change in every operation argument. Ledger status **values** in `rules.statuses`, entry status, status goals, and forbidden-status constraints must change together. Structural rule keys such as `pending`, `settled`, `void`, and action opcodes stay unchanged. A blanket recursive string/key replacement risks renaming semantic keys and must not define the implementation boundary.

The existing bounded semantic normalization must map every declared namespace bijection to the same fingerprint. Keep its world-size limits; do not remove factorial bounds merely to support larger lexicons. Apply the same split custody to every goal, vocabulary presentation, counterfactual, and rendering of one parent. Vocabulary variation is not a new independent world.

For a causal vocabulary diagnostic, create a DEV familiar-vocabulary control and a DEV reserved-vocabulary variant over the same ledger task. Mark the control as `familiar_control` in nonmodel metadata; never pool its known TRAIN strings into a claim that all DEV vocabulary is unseen. Only the `reserved_dev` stratum is the lexical holdout. Both remain DEV and use the same three-account/two-entry structural reservation and magnitudes. Account/entry/status namespaces are all exercised in this ledger arm; C alone cannot test ledger status terminology.

## V1 compatibility and archived profile validation

The current compatibility blocker is explicit. `auditors/integrity.py:validate_schema` reads only `configs/curriculum.json`, compares each record's `config_hash/config_version/generator_version` with that file, and reconstructs split/template rules from it. `pipelines/worker.py:process` calls the current default `generate_group(seed, split=split)` twice. Merely replacing the active curriculum with v2 would reject v1 provenance and reproduce old jobs using the wrong generator/configuration. Existing batch manifests' `config` object records operational budgets, not the complete curriculum identified by each record.

Preserve these v1 identities exactly:

- Wire schema `gcdl-1.0`; generator `generator-1.0`; curriculum `curriculum-1.0`; environment `environments-1.0`.
- V1 canonical curriculum digest, also stored in accepted record provenance: `d98651723bc9469105627dc55d78f0217eea67b98428020a5b1552452b4ca2f2`.
- V1 curriculum file-byte SHA-256: `c16450547db433d008001e4fd182978697c5b7e8029a2956e9f21145054c3780`. This differs from the canonical JSON digest because the file is formatted; validators must not confuse the two.
- V1 `generators/build.py` SHA-256: `78387f73de0bd8cdda4cbef446d9e735edfea175badb1575bb567fb087e4a575`.

The minimal compatible boundary is an allowlisted generation-profile resolver. An immutable registry resolves the exact tuple `(config_version, config_hash, generator_version, environment_version)` to a trusted archived curriculum and a statically selected versioned generator adapter. Validate the archive's canonical digest before using it, then reconstruct reserved sizes, template/lineage names, bounds, and supported vocabulary policy from that resolved profile. Unknown tuples, changed archive bytes, a version/hash mismatch, or unsupported environments fail closed; do not fall back to the current default or execute module/path text taken from an input record.

Keep the current v1 configuration and generator intact. A v1 archive copy and a separate v2 profile can live as flat files such as `configs/curriculum-v1.json` and `configs/curriculum-v2.json`, so the current top-level configuration hash enumeration includes them. The registry also belongs in that hashed configuration directory. A nested archive would require extending hash enumeration explicitly; silently unhashed archival files are not acceptable. The name `generator-2.0` and `curriculum-2.0` are proposed identities, not present implementations.

Each job must select a generation profile explicitly and use that same profile/adapter for generation, deterministic rerun, validation, and acceptance receipts. Preserve the original accepted-source code hash map in the original record and batch manifest; do not rewrite it to the new review's source map. A new validation/recheck receipt can identify its current checker snapshot separately. Config selection affects validation; it does not alter the v1 record ID formula, existing oracle evidence, utility ordering, fingerprints, or canonical bytes.

Version must also be unambiguous in operational identity. Current jobs are keyed only by `(seed, split)` and canonical batch names have decimal seeds. The in-memory pilot avoids persistence entirely. A later publishing design must bind jobs and manifests to their selected profile, reject a replay with a different profile, and allocate globally fresh decimal seeds so existing source-path authentication remains compatible. If simultaneous profiles are scheduled, extend the job key/profile state deliberately rather than reinterpret legacy jobs. Original v1 filenames, manifests, ledger rows, source IDs, and derivatives remain readable through their existing identities.

No wire-schema bump is needed for this experiment if input/target structures remain unchanged and goal-group/lexicon/version diagnostics stay in already permitted metadata/provenance. Schema documentation and executable version validation still need updating. New model-visible fields or altered target semantics would require a separately reviewed schema boundary; putting `goal_index`, candidate role, or intended winner in model input is forbidden. Environmental and oracle versions can remain v1 only while their mathematical semantics are unchanged.

## Minimal future code/config boundary

| Boundary | Proposed future responsibility | Required compatibility |
|---|---|---|
| New versioned generator module | Bounded endpoint-path proposals, fixed three-goal bundles, namespace-aware bijections, new v2 identities | Do not mutate v1 generator output or global version constants |
| Generation-profile registry/resolver and archived curricula | Resolve exact trusted config/version tuples and split reservations | V1 hash resolves to its original curriculum; unknown or tampered profiles reject |
| `auditors/integrity.py` version/template validation | Resolve record-specific profile, verify goal-bundle and namespace declarations, retain exact mathematical checks | Existing v1 records validate without rewriting their provenance |
| `pipelines/worker.py` generation/replay/job selection | Explicit profile for both runs and receipts; preserve resource/quarantine gates | Legacy jobs cannot replay under v2 by default |
| Optional v2 rendering adapter | Shared parent permutation across goal-only comparisons | Existing v1 rendered version and bytes remain unchanged |
| Focused compatibility/shortcut checks and research receipt | Paired parent-level metrics, finite exclusions, archived-profile checks | No model claims; no sealed optimization; fresh independent review before promotion |

The oracles, environment transitions, utility definition, rational label representation, record ID algorithm, and semantic fingerprint meaning should remain unchanged. Do not prefix fingerprints with a generator version to hide cross-version duplicates. New tasks can have new record IDs while semantically duplicated worlds remain detectable.

## Pre-registered bounded DEV protocol

Reserve fresh Arm C DEV seeds **420000–420031 inclusive**: 32 attempted parents, at most 24 endpoint proposals per seed, six supplied candidates, maximum plan length four, and three goal tasks per valid parent. No replacement seeds. The maximum is 96 canonical tasks. Reserve separate Arm V DEV seeds **421000–421031 inclusive**: 32 ledger parents, one familiar control and one reserved-DEV bijection per parent, maximum 64 tasks. These ranges are distinct from the earlier 410000 position controls, live million-range production, and 880000 sealed seeds. The combined pilot ceiling is 160 tasks, CPU only, with a five-minute wall-time cap, one worker, no model downloads, and no publication in the current resource pause.

Before implementation, freeze a machine-readable experiment specification containing these seeds, proposal order, attempt caps, endpoint/candidate rule, costs, full-goal weighting, lexical pools and their digests, version resolver, intended source snapshot, and the exact heuristic definition. Do not tune the lexicon, exclusions, proposal count, or seed list after observing DEV shortcut results. If a deterministic cap is inadequate, record the failed experiment and pre-register a distinct future specification rather than silently increase the budget.

The primary Arm C baseline is the existing v1 goal-blind heuristic on the matched v1 C record for each of these fresh seeds; no measured baseline for this seed range is claimed yet. Its after-measurement is the same heuristic on the three v2 goal tasks, scored for chosen-ID membership in the full exact best set. Also report exact tied-set recovery, a fixed-first-option selector, shortest/longest-plan selectors, and lowest opaque ID. These secondary diagnostics are fixed before evaluation and cannot certify the absence of every shortcut.

Required mechanical criteria:

1. Zero primary/independent disagreement; zero incorrect stored targets; deterministic regeneration under each profile; exact nonmodel source/parent custody and no input leakage.
2. For every valid C bundle, identical world/rules/constraints/ordered candidates across its three tasks; three distinct full-forest goals, all non-initial; a feasible goal-reaching candidate for each goal; pairwise disjoint exact best sets. Within model input, only the goal differs; canonical record IDs, record indices, goal metadata, and recomputed oracle/decision targets appropriately differ outside input.
3. Across valid equal-weight C triplets, the established deterministic goal-blind selector is unchanged within a triplet and has at most one-third best-membership correctness. Report its v1 before count, v2 after count, all denominators, per-parent results, and ties. Do not count renderings as independent tasks for statistical uncertainty.
4. Arm V bijections are total/injective over the active namespaces, noncolliding with structural/opcode strings, and consistent at every reference. Normalized fingerprints/outcomes and exact decision labels match their familiar controls. Every reserved-DEV surface string is absent from TRAIN reserved strings; report control and holdout strata separately.
5. All 1,936 currently nonsealed accepted v1 rows remain byte-identical and readable/validatable through the archived v1 resolver. Reproduce a bounded predeclared sample of v1 train/dev/ood batches with the frozen v1 generator/configuration and compare IDs, canonical candidate bytes, labels, and fingerprints. Reject fabricated config hashes and tampered archives. Existing seal/freeze receipts and exclusion policy remain unchanged; this phase does not open sealed labels to prove compatibility.

## Exclusions and stopping rules

Log every attempted seed, proposal count, and reason before aggregation. If 24 proposals do not yield three distinct non-initial endpoints, exclude the whole C parent with `insufficient_distinct_noninitial_endpoints`; retain its diagnostic receipt. Duplicate semantic parents, invalid forests, namespace collisions, or unsupported profile tuples are separately counted. A candidate's legal reversal failure is an ordinary target outcome, not an exclusion. A cheaper prefix or a tie is retained, not removed to improve the heuristic contrast.

If supposedly distinct full-goal best sets overlap, or if either oracle disagrees, preserve the whole bundle as quarantine evidence and fail the experiment; do not discard the mismatch from denominators. Do not publish two of a three-goal parent if one task fails. For the lexical arm, one failed mapping quarantines both members. Any changed v1 accepted byte, sealed access, nondeterministic reproduction, resource-ceiling violation, or leakage failure stops the pilot.

Require at least 24 valid C parents of 32 attempts to interpret the one-third diagnostic beyond a trivial handful; fewer means the pre-registered construction was inadequate, not a passing improvement. This threshold is a feasibility gate, not a statistical guarantee. Report observed exclusion rates and uncertainty at the parent level; the structural one-third bound follows from disjoint best sets, while empirical coverage of possible worlds remains narrow.

## Interpretation and next decision

This design removes the measured correlation between one recognizable constructed path and the sole supplied goal. It still supplies a finite closed candidate universe and goals copied from executable endpoints, with synthetic symbol vocabularies and small forests. A goal-aware shortcut based on terminal-action structure, partial goal overlap, or fixed generation grammar may remain. String holdouts do not test human language ambiguity or pretraining contamination. Shared code/specification ancestry can still correlate oracle errors despite separate executable checkers.

Success would justify a fresh independent review of this specific generator/config/version change and its mechanical measurements. It would not demonstrate learned planning, model retention, transfer to new environments, realistic decision uncertainty, or global optimality. The approved surface renderer and existing v1 corpus remain separate from this unimplemented future hypothesis. Implementation, production scheduling, or publishing requires a subsequent concrete reviewed change after the resource gate permits it.
