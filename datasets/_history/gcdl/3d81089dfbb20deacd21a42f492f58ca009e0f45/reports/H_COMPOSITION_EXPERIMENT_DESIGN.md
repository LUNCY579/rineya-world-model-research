# Register operation composition: DEV confirmation design

This is an inactive research prototype, not a canonical generator profile. Existing accepted v1/v2 shards, labels, IDs, split reservations, environment semantics and sealed instruments are unchanged. The current production selector remains v2. The experiment does not launch training, inference services or model downloads.

The archived composition audit found 66 one-step register H examples among 1,936 nonsealed v1 records. Supplied register operations were add and noop; all plans had one step and one goal atom. A valid positive singleton-add selector recovered every register optimum. The wider H audit's goal/constraint/rule-blind shortcut recovered 264/264 rows. V2 retains these non-C dynamics. These findings motivate a stronger register construction; they are not model performance measurements.

Exploratory hand witnesses and two independent algebraic challenges preceded this design. Those checks rejected a nonfunctional swap at the proposed last parameter and redundant simultaneous goals. The recorded developmental witnesses are separate from the fixed confirmation cohort. No sealed per-item result was used.

## Frozen hypotheses and cohort

Use exactly 32 frozen parameter settings with DEV seed identities 430000 through 430031, with no replacement seeds or rejection search. Numeric dynamics derive from index; seeds vary only opaque IDs and candidate order. Developmental indices overlap some confirmation dynamics, so these are mechanical confirmation cases rather than an unseen semantic holdout or 32 independent statistical samples. Each seed has a goal triplet and a trajectory-constraint quartet. All siblings within either bundle retain identical initial states, rules, candidate IDs, candidate order and available plans. Change only the declared goal or constraint field. IDs and order depend on seed and arm, never labels or goal index. The goal arm has eleven candidates; the constraint arm has nine. Both use three integer registers and a horizon of at most three, with legal bounds [-256,256].

The goal arm uses x=2+i%16, y=21+i, m=2+i//16 and initial (a,b,c)=(x,y,0). P executes swap(a,b), multiply(a,m), copy(a,c); Q reverses the first two operations; R moves the copy before its producer. Complete endpoint goals are (my,x,my), (y,mx,y), and (my,x,x). Their unique supplied optima are P, Q and R, each cost3. Set costs1 in this arm. Eight distinct two-set partial-goal distractors cover all nine single-atom omissions; the shared a/b partial plan is deduplicated. Each goal atom differs from its initial value. Removing any one goal atom lets a cheaper cost2 partial plan beat the original optimum. The construction therefore tests order, data dependence and joint necessity of the supplied goals rather than merely counting goal atoms.

The constraint arm uses initial a=2+i%8, b=3+i//8, c=6+i, goal a=2c-2 and b=2c. P temporarily raises a to2c, copies it to b, then subtracts2; it costs3. R doubles c, copies c to b, and sets a to2c-2; it costs6. Q sets a and b directly; Q' reverses those independent sets, both cost8. Set costs4 here. Three partial-goal distractors and P's prefix make both goal atoms necessary without displacing any complete-goal winner.

The four constraint conditions are none, maximum a≤2c-2, protected c, and their conjunction. Exact best sets must be {P}, {R}, {P}, and {Q,Q'}, respectively. Initial constraints are satisfied. The maximum constraint rejects P at intermediate steps1/2 despite its acceptable terminal a; protection rejects R because c changes. Both primitives are necessary for the conjunction's optimum. None/protection is a negative winner-flip control. Dropping goal a without constraints creates the legitimate cost2 tie between P's prefix and R's multiply-copy prefix; all diagnostics retain complete tied sets.

## Measurement and refusal criteria

Freeze the exact specification, executable source/configuration map and every base input commitment before measurement. Create a durable once-only STARTED receipt before any confirmation oracle calls. Interrupted attempts cannot rerun; failures remain immutable. Both separately implemented oracles must agree exactly on complete trajectories, costs, constraints and rational labels. The authoritative decision checker validates rankings and ties. Reconstruct each bundle twice and require identical bytes.

The 800-case ceiling counts 96 goal tasks, 128 constraint tasks, 544 single-goal-atom ablations and 32 baseline H register tasks. All work has a 300-second wall bound and a one-MiB result ceiling. Baseline generation also expands 32 full v2 groups, executing its oracles on other families; the report discloses these additional generated rows separately. Only the selected H rows count in the baseline comparison. The baseline is fresh generation at the fixed DEV seeds, not a read of accepted or sealed gold.

Require all32 parents, zero oracle/label/reproduction mismatches, 96 three-step goal winners, 32 exact conjunction ties, all six operations supplied across the arms, and 544 nonempty changed optima after atom ablation. Within each goal triplet every fixed single-choice goal-blind selector has a one-third ceiling. A constraint-blind choice of P recovers exactly64/128 quartet choices; terminal-only constraint checking also recovers64/128 and fails the binding intermediate maximum cases. Complete-set recovery and selected-best membership remain distinct in reports.

The result retains per-input, oracle, decision and ablation hashes; exact best sets; winning lengths; candidate endpoint/utility/violation summaries; baseline record commitments; and the frozen source and STARTED identities. A result-body integrity digest catches accidental modification. The independent final review must additionally pin the exact result-file hash; structural validation alone is not a reviewer signature. No canonical shards or training exports are published by this experiment.

## What confirmation can support

A PASS supports these finite mechanical construction properties and a reviewed basis for a later versioned generator proposal. It does not establish learned generalization, generic composition, global action-space optimality or the absence of formula-aware shortcuts. The cross-version baseline changes candidate count, goals and plan construction; the clean causal contrasts are within the fixed goal and constraint bundles. A production v3 profile, TRAIN primitives, reserved OOD conjunctions, ledger H improvements and a new independent confirmation instrument require separate implementation, split design and review.
