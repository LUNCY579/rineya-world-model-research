## Original recovery design rationale (now implemented)

The four-step cold rank-4 pilot did not establish a viable new response interface.
It is insufficient to decide H1/H2, and the twelve-step longer-training control
also failed accepted prediction. The original rank-16 all-projection registered
BWM F passed6/6 original-format constructed cases. A useful next development
study must start from a functioning matched forward interface/capacity, rather
than treat cold undertraining as evidence against predictive learning.

Before new native measurements, append a recovery protocol specifying:

1. An explicit pure formatter around the validated goal-blind RegisterWorld
   forward prompt/parser. Mapping predicted x/legal/terminated to the canonical
   state interface may derive failure from PREDICTED legality, never exact rules.
2. A common unmerged registered-F initialization for A0/A1/A2 and controls, with
   exact adapter hash/rank/targets recorded. No dense merge or owner-file changes.
3. A permitted-training exposure audit from existing TRAIN keys/provenance only;
   no historical sealed bank access. Fresh development must include nondegenerate
   legal changes and boundaries, and distinguish seen transitions from OOD limits.
4. A larger but bounded development pilot with identical seeds/initialization,
   adequate schema qualification, balanced query budgets and explicit extra-label/
   capacity controls. Use new data/output/config/freeze paths and label all
   development selection as exploratory.
5. Separate h1/h2/h4 direct and own-prediction rollout scoring. Preserve the
   existing decoder/schema/refusal policy per instrument. Do not repair benchmark
   outputs after inspecting correctness.

Only a useful, stable recovery pilot can authorize G4. Then construct exact-
verified TRAIN-only plans, run A3/A4 students with/without search, perform native
ledger/Blocksworld and stress evaluation, and establish behavioral retention.
Freeze a complete new confirmation registration and at least three training
seeds before any confirmation labels are accessed. Existing v1 confirmation
labels remain protected.
