## Explicit unresolved evidence

- **Confirmation/replication:** the old pilot vault is24 mixed-domain rows from19
  parents, with no planning goals; its cold proposed family is not warm H3
  confirmation. Sealing alone proves neither novelty nor native-format eligibility.
  After a qualified pilot, at least three independent training seeds are preferred;
  reusing one warm seed with several continuations measures continuation variability
  only. Shared parents/seeds require paired/crossed units, not seed-times-case IID.
- **Domains/generalization:** current positive native prediction evidence is
  RegisterWorld only. Ledger status/key/tick and Block rename/representation
  cases are frozen inputs, not outcomes. Object counts/longer sequences, unseen
  rule combinations and held-out goal families remain unmeasured. Tiny Block
  canonical parents are finite; renamed copies cannot inflate independence.
- **Causal ablations:** MTP4/shared modules/weights/shuffled targets, full-versus-
  delta/mixed, legality-head/rollout-loss and verification contrasts lack native
  recovery results. Copy/first-last-action/prompt-cue signatures are posthoc
  observations. Any resource-feasible subset needs prospective causal controls,
  without repairing/redrawing frozen results to manufacture benefit.
- **Secondary/retention:** six behavior cases do not establish broad preservation;
  inherited F fails all three Ledger cases and A2 already loses one Register
  boundary case. R/P baseline/adapted behavior is still pending. Boolean flags
  and malformed-only selective risk are not probability calibration. Current
  normalized excess path cost excludes unsolved cases; it is not all-case decision
  regret. Physical GPU/FLOP and broader latency evidence are also absent.
  A new additive conditional-legality calibration driver passed18 semantic tests,
  independent review, actual tokenizer/lineage audits and a72-member prospective
  freeze; see [its preparation report](LEGAL_CALIBRATION_PREPARATION_2026_10_12.md).
  It has no native probabilities or calibration result and cannot certify
  naturally generated state/planning confidence.
- **H4/H5:** prerequisites remain conditional. Standard KV reuse, lower admission
  floors and singleton batching do not establish expert-routing, compression or
  MLA benefit; A0 singleton lowers working set while slowing elapsed time and
  increasing physical forwards. Preserve that tradeoff and A2's resource refusal.
