# Confirmation compatibility audit — 2026-10-12

The existing pilot-v1 vault does not supply primary confirmation for a warm H3
seeded study under the current registration. The old vault remains closed. This
audit read registrations, public manifests and builder source only; it opened
no sealed labels, sealed tasks or historical evaluation banks, and ran no model.
Native H3 student/control outcomes are unknown at this audit's cutoff. Nothing
here asserts that their pilot gates pass, changes a protocol or authorizes access.

The pilot manifest records 24 confirmation dynamics rows from 19 parent worlds,
with no goals, no guaranteed novel rule-composition withholding and disjointness
checked within that collection only (`dsv3wm/datasets/pilot-v1/manifest.json:5`,
`:48`, `:89`, `:91`, `:95`). The builder rotates three domains and permits
different trajectories from a same-split parent; semantic trajectory deduplication
does not make every row an independent parent (`dsv3wm/src/dsv3wm/data.py:195`,
`:208`, `:222`, `:224`). The bank therefore cannot supply H3 planning outcomes
or be treated as 24 independent confirmation units.

Warm exposure changes the question. Registered F covers all 1,455 ID one-step
keys at limits 32/48/64: newly composed ID trajectories are not unseen state/action
tests (`dsv3wm/prereg/RECOVERY_G3_V1.md:61`). The original builder samples ordinary
RegisterWorld limits 24/32/40 and does not exclude predecessor training keys
(`dsv3wm/src/dsv3wm/domains.py:242`). The later Ledger manifest records exclusion
of 501 known T2 TRAIN parents/operative keys while declaring historical evaluation
and dense-parent exposure unknown (`dsv3wm/datasets/ledger-recovery-v1/manifest.json:50`,
`:60`, `:200`). The old vault's five-field Ledger events also omit original T2's
neutral `tick` field (`dsv3wm/src/dsv3wm/domains.py:283`). Cryptographic sealing
alone establishes neither warm-adapter novelty nor native-format compatibility.

The old proposed confirmation family is P1 free-running four-step state accuracy
and P2 learned-planner goal success, against A0 and A5: +5 percentage points on
both endpoints against both controls, four paired tests with Holm correction,
seed 0 artifact testing and seeds 1/2 replication. Bank size, power, cohorts and
other fields remain pending; that file explicitly is not a complete G5
registration (`dsv3wm/prereg/PILOT_V1.md:82`, `:92`, `:102`, `:106`). These proposed
cold-pilot confirmation criteria do not automatically become an H3 claim family.

H3 instead requires the registered `{"actions":[...]}` proposal, at most six
actions, all legal execution and a final `finish` terminating at the desired
goal. Pure greedy top-1, fixed-B0 learned reranking and offline pass@4 have
different interpretations (`dsv3wm/prereg/RECOVERY_H3_DRAFT_V1.md:74`, `:235`,
`:256`). A3/C3 share the same final A2 warm artifact, and A4/C4 the same final A0
artifact. Their controls match nominal updates/forwards, not labels, token work
or measured FLOPs (`dsv3wm/prereg/RECOVERY_H3_CONTROL_CLARIFICATION_V1.md:8`, `:19`,
`:33`). These contrasts test the planning-distillation package versus repeated
state CE; they do not separately isolate verification or multi-horizon transfer.

The **already registered operational H3 gates** are at least 9/12 valid greedy
proposals, paired net top-1 gain at least 2/12 versus the student's own warm
parent, no lost matched reranking success, ID h4 paired net loss at most 2/12,
and behavior/runtime guards. They authorize only larger development if satisfied,
not efficacy, equivalence or confirmation. The draft explicitly requires matched
controls, practical thresholds, a claim family and a fresh sealed bank before
confirmation (`dsv3wm/prereg/RECOVERY_H3_DRAFT_V1.md:275`, `:281`, `:286`).

The following are **proposed future prerequisites**, not additional registered
pilot thresholds:

- Fix the selected question, endpoints, contrasts, practical effect thresholds,
  multiplicity, retention margins, bank size/power and failure/selection rules
  before confirmation access. State whether P1 is superiority or a retention
  guard. Keep own-warm and C3/C4 contrasts visible; never substitute pass@4 for
  deployment top-1 success or inherit the old four-test family implicitly.
- Predeclare at least three adaptation seeds and pair initialization, corpus,
  draws and sampling within each seed. For full-pipeline variability, rerun the
  warm A0/A2 stage and its H3 study/control continuations for every seed. Reusing
  one seed-0 warm artifact with several continuation seeds measures continuation
  variability only. A fixed predecessor F/T2 artifact remains a conditional
  initialization. Analyze shared parents and training seeds as crossed units,
  without treating seed-times-case observations as independent replications.
- Construct a new terminal-aware planning bank and informative four-step dynamics
  bank. With six actions including `finish`, primary goals must need at most five
  moves. Freeze goal/difficulty/rule cohorts and each interface's eligibility;
  the old mixed-domain bank cannot silently become a RegisterWorld H3 benchmark.
- Reserve one primary episode per canonical parent and exclude every prior
  public parent, including the old confirmation registry, recovery TRAIN/DEV/
  invalid parents, all teacher-pool parents, and relevant exposed development/
  retention parents. Group renamed, reordered, inert-tick and goal variants
  together. Use SHA-bound predecessor input-key audits or permitted blind
  exclusion attestations where available; do not open historical sealed banks
  to construct exclusions. Label ID composition and unknown prior exposure
  honestly rather than claiming all held-out inputs were never encountered.
- Freeze source/config/adapter/corpus hashes, common query/position accounting,
  cache and decoding policies, independent scoring and all completed artifacts
  before access. Report realized costs, failed searches and teacher costs;
  equal caps or nominal forwards do not establish equal compute.

No data, freeze, label access or current protocol modification follows from this
note. Preserve the old vault and pilot records; any larger study needs its own
prospective registration and a separately qualified access decision.
